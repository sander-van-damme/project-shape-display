Baseline: Bambu Lab X1 Carbon with PLA, normally 0.4 mm nozzle; 0.2 mm optional for fine features. Recorded build volume: 256 × 256 × 256 mm, requiring modular construction for the ~400 mm map.

Qualification: published sensing/motion resolution is not finished-part accuracy. No universal tolerance or minimum reliable feature is qualified. Test critical sliding/rotating/snap/press fits with clearance-matrix coupons on the actual process. A 0.20 mm wall remains experimental even with a 0.2 mm nozzle.

Record nozzle, layer height, filament/lot, orientation and XY/hole compensation. Material changes require friction, shrinkage, fit and printability requalification. PLA creep/fatigue matter; color alone is not a strength specification. Do not assume particle-filled filament works with a 0.2 mm nozzle.
