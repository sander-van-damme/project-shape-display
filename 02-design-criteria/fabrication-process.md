**Manufacturing allocation:** prioritize inexpensive, easily repeated, error-tolerant rods; do not require thousands of near-perfect tiny features. Where only a few shared actuator/selector components need tighter accuracy, compare calibrated local printing with outsourced precision/metal printing, machining, suitable mold-and-casting processes or other manufacturing routes. A 3D-printed mold does not automatically tolerate molten-metal temperatures or yield a precision casting; assess materials, release, shrinkage, tooling life and total economics. Precision concentrated in a few accessible pieces can be justified, but repeated sensitive joints (including threads) still need board-scale fit, wear, assembly and repair accounting.
