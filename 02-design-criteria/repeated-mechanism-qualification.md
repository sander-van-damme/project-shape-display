Gate: what must work correctly 6,400 times? Minimize precision/compliant contacts per cell; prefer simple passive columns plus accessible shared mechanisms.

Avoid: single-extrusion moving features, force-critical tiny springs, repeated submillimetre precision, friction hold where hard stops are possible, tight global tolerances and undetectable unrecoverable cell faults. Prefer positive engagement, compression load paths, generous clearances, accessible replaceable parts and local recovery.

Audit each architecture: repeated moving parts, precision contacts/cell, compliant elements, wear interfaces/service life, tolerance-sensitive interactions, correlated and single-cell failures, and service access. Label the evidence for each.

Prototype ladder: single cell → small full-pitch array (e.g. 5×5) → complete bank/module → multiple banks → full machine. Validate fit, neighbor interaction, load, repeated cycling, reset, correlated failures and timing before scaling. A calculated or CAD pass is not physical qualification.
