# Park equality-decoded command memory; investigate selective load stops

**Decision:** conclude the retained multilevel command/decoding campaign with
scoped exclusions. Do not develop another local latch or reversible cam for
the tested package. No complete machine is accepted. Prioritize selective
load-bearing height memory, retaining direct positioning as the simple control.
This concludes one campaign, not the research program or multilevel memory as
a physical principle. Engineering execution and task structure belong in Paperclip.

**Basis:** E-089 establishes fewer writes and supported abstract controllers.
E-091 rejects the 41-command positive lock under its composed error bounds;
its nine-level section survives conditionally. E-092 adds finite unloading,
199.6-mm elevator travel and separate-reader necessity. E-093's long bridge
loses engagement and its captured cam cannot reset while retaining deposited
terrain. Independent reader dwell fixes equality timing, not load retention.
The embodiment already needs ≥44,800 local sliders before those missing outputs.

E-094 couples distinct setting costs, support/reader motion, proof, retry,
parking, full/regional workload and complete-channel allowances. At the declared
central allocation, four banks need <0.48482-s memory rounds to reach <30 s;
eight banks allow <1.00726 s but leave <$0.3765 per complete channel at $250
elsewhere and no cell purchases. Selective deck's four-bank stop-setting
allowance is <0.76346 s and its corresponding channel allowance <$0.7622.
These are conditional offers; different operation costs or motion reverse
rankings. No price, manufacturability or reliability pass follows.

**Consequences:** preserve the source and unique exclusion witnesses. Stop
nominal equality, lock-width, slotted-bridge and captured-cam refinements and
do not print the failed package. Keep separate positive supports held on failed
proof; do not credit repeated readback against common bias. Neither binary-mask
references nor direct heads inherit qualified machinery from favorable budgets.

The next architectural question is whether selective pickup plus load-bearing
height memory can combine state storage and support with fewer local functions.
Investigate actual hooks, unloaded setting, approach/deposit/withdrawal and
unchanged-cell passage, with grounded modular structure. Compare existing stepped
rotors with changed load-path/support principles and direct heads; internal
selector pitch is not constrained to surface pitch. The historical rotor coupon,
perimeter-only sheet and long tiny shaft are not prescribed solutions.

**Reopening and residual risk:** reopen retained-command work only with a
materially changed finite output retention/disengagement or decoding principle,
robust complete setting/reading at the proposed resolution, and recomputed
budgets meeting product limits under declared uncertainty. Better equality alone
or an assumed free latch does not qualify. Selective stops may fail packing,
load transfer, cost or regional isolation; the priority is reversible and should
terminate on a decisive mechanism contradiction. Hardware qualification, process
priors, purchased BOM, durability and physical readback remain future evidence.
