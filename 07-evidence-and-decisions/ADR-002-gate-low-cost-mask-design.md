Decision: Retain the banked low-cost mask architecture as a conditional alternative, with no unconditional readiness claim.
Basis: Corrected historical model: $226.77 purchased, $263.05 with assumed 1.16 delivery uplift, 11.956 s mechanism update. Correct leaf bending section reduces release force estimate from 0.160 to 0.020 N; positive stops must supply holding capacity. It has no per-cell readback.
Consequence: Require true-pitch fit, release/hold force, wear, jam containment and loaded regional-disturbance evidence. Count offline mask preparation when judging an unannounced map change; mechanism cycle alone is insufficient.
Residual risk: A small zero-failure coupon cannot qualify board reliability. Common platen motion, unsupported loaded updates, mask preparation and silent jams remain product limitations. A $250 purchased target differs from a $250 delivered estimate; never interchange them. Current source assumptions and historical BOM costs are unverified physically.

Selection priority is superseded by ADR-009. Retain these timing/cost and missing-generator lessons as historical comparison evidence; a new investigation requires a distinct decision-relevant question.
