# Prioritize simple columns and shared physical programming

Primary research now favors simple repeated columns and shared, reusable,
accessible mechanical programming: masks, encoded layers and common support.
E-208–E-215 local toe/return/capture/guidance refinement is dormant. Their
historical “next” suggestions are not an active agenda. Reopening requires a
concrete change that removes the repeated manufacturing burden and a competitive
whole-machine ledger; another nominal clearance result is insufficient.
Canonical evidence and executable sources remain intact.

E-216/E-217 park resident shutter stacks and reusable row-mask programmers:
25,600 resident support switches or hundreds of controlled writer gates, with
unresolved supported pickup and retained-state mechanisms. E-218 retains an
ideal positive two-cassette handoff (raise target, then lower old), eliminating
old-code writing and friction grips. Its translating staircase cannot pack;
E-219's rotary escape still needs six depth bands in favorable bounds, while
solid blocks need 38,320 pieces and hundreds of handlers. These scoped
embodiments remain parked; preserve the support laws and reproducible bounds.

E-220 constructs in-place crossed redundant combs: either row or column support
holds a simple height-storing column. This avoids individual releases and cassette
inventory, but the row/delta programmer needs 1,280 selectable column bars plus
1,280 reusable fingers in its favorable sampled schedule. Park that embodiment;
neither separable shared backing nor rectangle release supplies free independent
state or throughput.

E-221's one-visit absolute head removes those selectors/delta groups, but needs
240 independently driven/arrested shoes even in its favorable stopped scenario.
Continuous 2-mm passage needs 640 and adds sliding contact; park it. A common
sweep restores 6,400 resident catches; it does not earn local refinement.
Keep the stopped row head only as a cost/arrest comparator. E-222's retained
shaft couplings plus grounded parking dogs supply an ideal supported switching
sequence, but stopped transfers need five heads/400 channels. Its explicit
optimistic $807 allocation and $950.40 for 240 catalog actuators reject those
procurement embodiments. Shared hydraulic shutoff does not retain differential
shoe positions. Flat-stack exchange removes absolute axes but the tested feeder
needs 640 forks, 640 shuttles and 25,600 tokens; park it before local refinement.
E-223 adds a separate obstruction: prewritten relief translated by at least one
pitch cannot preserve unequal unchanged neighbors sharing its loaded run. Below-rail
return and an ideal grounded brake do not repair that material-coordinate conflict.
Separate five-code cell loops escape it but restore 6,400 retained phases, 12,800
pulleys and 32,000 pads; park the specified layout before keeper/contact refinement.
E-224 decouples writing from loaded support using compact vertical bars and a
common cassette comb. Its finite branching-rail writer still needs 1,024 gates
at zero error, or 2,560 in the ±.35-mm scenario; park that embodiment. Preserve
the finite-follower gate-occupancy bound and compact encoding, not a machine
selection. Reopening needs changed media handling/programming that removes this
throughput burden and constructs supported extraction, transport and exchange.
E-225 parks the indexed-feed variant too: stopping removes the continuous-speed
limit but retains narrow absolute phase margins and 1,024 gates in the favorable
45-mm schedule, with captured followers and split routing unresolved. Plain
bar-bottom writing collides with upper rails. End branching-rail refinement;
reopen only through a changed shared programming transaction, not a smaller toe
or faster assumed stroke. No selected drive or complete-machine physical pass.

E-226 removes loaded writer retention by orienting unloaded bars horizontally,
then capturing them with a common comb before turnover. Park its bank-owned
writer layout: finite rotation needs 113-mm clearance travel; even granted
routing requires 20 heads/1,600 selectors/26.995 s. Separating overlapping bays
on depth levels gives a best sampled 85.001 s. Preserve the unloaded-orientation
principle and explicit transport burden, not further finger/pad/comb refinement.
Any shared-writer reopening must supply buffers, turnover occupancy and delivery;
notch engagement alone also fails to establish height after upright seating.

Every deep mechanism investigation must first justify its system-level value
against a simpler comparator, including replication, assembly, correlated process
errors, service, arbitrary-map time and purchased-cost allocations. Unknown
manufacturing capability cannot count as a pass. Narrow repeated margins or
unaffordable channel multiplicity redirect work immediately; they are not merely
report caveats. This changes research priority, not product constraints or the
need for eventual physical qualification.
