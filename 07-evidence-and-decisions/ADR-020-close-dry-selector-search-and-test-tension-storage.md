# Close dry shared-selector search; investigate tension-based local storage

**Decision:** conclude the studied dry positive-support/shared-selection campaign
without an admitted complete selector or machine. Retire dependent whole-machine
economics for this branch. Keep crossbolt/powered-pawl sections, permanent power
follower, split command cage and long extraction sleeve as conditional building
blocks; stop synchronous common-retractor refinement. No print, purchase, product
requirement change or hardware acceptance follows. The earlier thermal campaign's
administrative recovery guard remains untouched.

**Basis:** E-115 retains finite support sections but does not establish selection.
E-116/117 find transfer and follower-grounding failures; E-118 separates the
permanent follower yet cannot independently extract its trapped dog. E-119's
opening cage fixes local release. Its narrow common retractor cannot acquire
mixed command heights; a wide one *can*, but common descent cannot simultaneously
restore disjoint command intervals. Closing a cage first obstructs incoming
collar entry by 1.1–1.7 mm in the declared box. These are scoped contradictions,
not proof that every dry support, clutch or sequential recapture mechanism fails.

Successive repairs increase repeated machinery without providing command
retention through proof, isolated jam recovery and positive mixed reset. The
remaining missing operation is a physical conditional capture/reset mechanism,
not a tolerance refinement. Full-machine timing/BOM claims would lack a required
selector input. Do not infer feasibility from cancelling that downstream work.

**Portfolio comparison:** another conditional split-jaw drive might escape, but
would retain the very command/return burden that this campaign failed to resolve.
E-120 tests a different load/storage principle: tension transmission to individually
retained drums, serviced by reusable rotary heads. Generated ideal two-edge routes
show that dedicated tendons are not excluded solely by member count, while their
740–881-m route length and 12,800 ends make perimeter storage unattractive as the
lead. A local two-turn drum seed has four disk-only planes versus sixteen for the
one-turn seed, but still needs finite return, lock and access. It receives bounded
exploration, not architecture selection. Direct rack heads remain the simple
comparator, with E-099's complete-channel affordability gap intact.

**Start/change/stop:** prioritize finite distributed drum/tendon support and
private torque takeover, comparing gravity return and antagonistic routing.
First resolve bidirectional reach and positive support through acquisition,
unlocking, arbitrary old-to-new motion, relocking/proof and withdrawal around
unchanged neighbors. Then admit only complete finite survivors to full/regional
scheduling, all repeated purchases/assembly and failure/readback accounting.
Do not proceed directly from disk counts to machine economics. Execution and
continuation belong in Paperclip; no second campaign plan is stored here.

**Reopening:** reopen the shared dry-selector branch only with an executable
materially changed conditional capture/reset topology, or complete accessible
private/transported drive escaping its demonstrated collisions with credible
channel/travel burden. A smaller error box, another fork gap or equal-friction
sweep is insufficient. Tension storage should stop or redirect on its own finite
contradiction; it has no immunity from long-cable, bend, preload, wear, creep,
service-volume and affordable-channel constraints.

**Residual limits:** bounds are deterministic epistemic scenarios, not X1C process
priors or reliability estimates. No complete qualified reader, finite shared
selector, purchased BOM, manufacturing yield, durability, full-board update or
regional-disturbance performance is established. Campaign closure is an evidence-
based allocation decision; the research program continues.
