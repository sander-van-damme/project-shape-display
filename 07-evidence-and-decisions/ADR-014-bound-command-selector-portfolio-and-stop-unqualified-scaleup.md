# Conclude the command-selector comparison without a product winner

**Decision:** close the finite magnetic/mechanical selector discrimination with
scoped exclusions and conditional reserves. None of the investigated packages
establishes a complete affordable, isolated, <30-s display. Stop parameter
refinement of the tested implementations; retain reproducible mechanisms,
failures and reopening bounds. This is campaign closure, not completion of
the research program or rejection of whole physical principles.

No strict whole-system Pareto dominance can be claimed among the reserves:
magnetic isolation/physical package and power remain unresolved, while positive
mechanical addressing relocates burden to keys, repeated fits and many complete
drives. The bought ceiling cannot be waived, and missing prices cannot count as
zero. E-083's force/strength/drag/mass boxes are not process priors or reliability
statistics. Positive time allowance is insufficient for selection.

## What changes in the program

Use the common-coordinate elevator and explicit zero-deposit/regrip or signed
displacement schedules from E-081. A rigid grip cannot reset unequal heights
simultaneously. For transient command storage, use E-083's actual mask changes
and final clear; selected-row counts alone are neither complete write counts nor
a universal lower bound. Reuse command storage only with independently retained
support outputs. Include physical preparation, clearing, readback, support proof,
retry and return within update time. Unknown initial command states inhibit cam
motion. No command-reader observation proves mechanical support by itself.

No fabrication or purchase follows. Further finite-field or nonlinear contact
work has low value until a candidate can describe a complete physical package
and an affordable addressing route; calibration of arbitrary damping would not
resolve that architectural gap. These are evidence conditions, not human
approval gates. Existing support/pawl evidence remains usable within its own
bounds; no rejected A-022 structural mechanism is rehabilitated.

The highest-value next opportunity is **shared-energy command programming that
reduces independently driven axes and repeated sliding keys together**, with an
explicit mechanical distribution/selection/reset path. Compare mechanically
commutated continuous transport or reusable strip writers against this campaign's
banked bound and a simple direct-drive comparator. A new campaign should first
generate materially different complete coupling topologies, reject conflicts
between arbitrary row patterns and shared motion, then carry survivors through
finite geometry, regional scheduling, force/cost and recovery. Do not assume
an address decoder, flying engagement, or one actuator broadcasting arbitrary
independent states is free. The old A-013 reusable-head schedule is a comparison
reference, not an automatically reopened solution. A candidate earns deeper
work by escaping a specific demonstrated burden, not by a renamed bank or lower
assumed friction.

Residual risks: no physical selector, lifetime/yield distribution, assembled
support-transfer mechanism, qualified reader, complete BOM or full-machine
performance is established. Reopen affected implementations when the table's
material input changes; preserve unaffected negative evidence. CTO owns the
next technical investigation and integration; no staffing, purchase or CEO
resource decision is needed for bounded computational search.
