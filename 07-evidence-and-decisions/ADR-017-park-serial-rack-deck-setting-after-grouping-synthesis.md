# Conclude selective pickup and height-memory synthesis with scoped exclusions

**Decision:** close this campaign without selecting a machine. Stop the tested
upright-stop, loose pickup-lock and serial rack-dog/deck implementations. Keep
conditional contact knowledge and the row-comb time/pickup-count tradeoff as
reopening evidence. Neither direct heads nor the older rotor gain product
acceptance by surviving as comparisons.

**Basis:** E-095's open contact separates after grounding, avoiding captured-hook
release. E-096's unpreloaded aperture lock cannot satisfy insertion and retained
web bounds together. E-097's translating staircase hits unchanged columns; its
carousel's clearance key admits a wrong-height collision. E-098 moves selection
to deck home and support memory to column racks, preserving contact conditionally
but requiring 18 dog-setting visits per row. E-099 proves that all contiguous
serial lift partitions lose full-map time under the tested motion laws, even
with free support-proof time; resident writers nearly preserve the same large
channel count as separate banks.

A 16-bank row comb reduces pickup pads from 6,400 to 1,280, but needs 28.943 s
at the central allocation and 1,312 complete shared channels. Its $0.19055
mean allowance with $250 elsewhere/free bought cell items is not a price.
Four-bank direct positioning gives 18.854 s and 320 complete heads under its
own unqualified boundary. Different functional channel prices could reverse
value; no universal dominance, manufacturing reliability or impossibility is
claimed. The concrete complexity/time evidence is sufficient to redirect the
next exploration allocation rather than detail another rack guide or latch.

**Consequences:** retain unique failure witnesses and executable sources; no
print, purchase or nominal lock/partition sweep. Reopen only with materially
changed support addressing that removes group visits, robust positive pickup/
stop retention escaping the failed geometry, or a complete affordable writer
with finite approach/withdrawal, individual proof and bounded recovery inside
recomputed full/regional budgets. A smaller model error box or faster assumed
slot alone does not establish that change.

**Next opportunity:** revisit hydraulic chamber-side mechanical coincidence
(A-014) as a distinct energy/state principle, beginning with crossed selector
rails and distributed moving support. Its half-select isolation and rail-force
failures have explicit reopening conditions; they must remain constraints.
This potentially replaces the rack embodiment's repeated height-group writes
with volume positioning, but repeats seals/valves and may lose on leakage,
force, manufacture or cost. Require actual selection, two-way energy transfer,
isolation and reset before channel-count credit. Compare positive-lock hybrids
and direct heads; do not grant cheap fluid seals or universal PLA accuracy.
Execution scope and continuation belong in Paperclip, not this repository.

Residual risks across the portfolio are complete affordable addressing, safe
lowering, physical verification, correlated manufacturing error, lifetime,
service access and full-machine qualification. This closes one investigation,
not the research program, and requires no CEO engineering sign-off.
