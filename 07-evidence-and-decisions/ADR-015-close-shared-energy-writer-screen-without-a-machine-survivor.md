# Close the shared-energy writer screen without selecting a machine

**Decision:** conclude the shared-transport/reusable-writer discrimination with
scoped rejections. None of the tested complete routes and local couplings earns
integrated-machine development. Preserve the useful nominal sections and changed
capture geometry; stop further nominal cam, fork, paddle and spring sweeps.
This concludes the campaign, not the research program or these entire physical
principles. No physical validation, purchased BOM or product winner is established.

| Hypothesis | Decisive evidence | Disposition and reopening input |
|---|---|---|
| Continuously transported programmable cams | E-084 sustained word-rate/dwell boundary; E-085 finite changeover and completion interference, registration/withdrawal overhead | Stop tested rigid changeover. Reopen with complete force-limited capture, independent withdrawal and an affordable gate/readback rate; buffering alone does not change sustained throughput |
| Stopped powered writer | E-086 opposed forks collide and staggered flush violates regional parking; E-087 short rotary paddles remove that flush; E-088 improves capture but its finite series joint has no force-envelope survivor and admits recoil collisions | Keep short rotary geometry as a comparator, not a machine. Reopen with materially changed joint/return or calibrated compliance and fit, plus a complete affordable data channel; raising a stress/force limit without product evidence is insufficient |
| Retained command carrier with fixed setter and shared working stroke | E-087 long-paddle independent setting collides; coplanar racetrack collides with parked neighbors; bank-confined ferry pays return per row | Stop those setter/return combinations. Reopen with changed folded/stacked setter and return topology, complete preparation/readback and joint accounting, not another turn-radius sweep |

Cam sections, fork staggering, paddle lengths and rod sizes are parameter or
packing variants, not separate architectures. The campaign added distinct
energy/memory/transport hypotheses and executable finite failure witnesses;
it did not discover an inexpensive physical decoder by counting address bits.

## Complete-system outcome

Every reference retains 6,400 command memories and independent output supports.
Moving the data mechanism to a reusable writer can eliminate E-083's captive
keys and long bars, but multiplies complete data drives by parallel bank count.
E-088's 20-bank reference needs 1,600 data axes and 40 shared lift/transport axes,
plus 3,200 bearings and 1,600 compliance/return couplings. Its 27.212-s reference
schedule at unqualified accelerations leaves <$0.1524 mean complete bought
channel allowance with $250 elsewhere, and 2.788 s for omitted behavior/retries.
Forty banks buy time with 3,200 data axes and halve that allowance. Eighty banks
collide in the finite reference package even under synchronous scanning.

Unknown motor/controller/reader prices are not zero, and a cost allowance is
not a quote or a proof of economic impossibility. No complete channel was shown
to fit. The stopped reference's conditional time reserve cannot rescue its
failed force/recoil package. The carrier's shared output energy does not remove
its setter throughput, retention, return, registration or recovery costs.
No whole-machine dominance or numerical manufacturing reliability is established.

Regional operation requires an actual idle-bank route. E-088 supplies a
conditional high-parking/home-return itinerary for its finite package;
lifting at the last row remains a collision. This preserves a useful changed
route despite the joint rejection. It adds vertical space and return work.
No unchanged-region displacement or miniature-retention performance is measured.

## Consequences and next opportunity

Keep output cams parked until command and individual withdrawal proofs succeed;
a common lift position or a spring return shoulder does not prove each blade
withdrew. Retain explicit mask clearing and all preparation inside update time.
Do not grant independence to repeated reads or average common rail/material
errors across thousands of cells. Positive simulations remain conditional on
retained supports, unqualified properties and the missing physical reader.

No print, purchase, additional staff or deeper contact solver is justified for
these failed embodiments. Printing another nominal paddle would not resolve
complete-channel economics, return-force bounds or state verification. Changed
inputs in the table can reopen a scoped implementation without erasing unaffected
failures. No CEO approval is required for these technical exclusions.

The next high-value architectural opportunity is to **reduce physical command
transactions or independently driven data axes before refining another dense
joint**. Compare a genuinely multilevel retained command/support mechanism against
the 43 binary-mask schedule and a simple direct positioning comparator. Explicitly
include arbitrary height replacement, lowering/reset, selection isolation,
energy/support transfer, persistent-memory verification and regional recovery.
E-088 inverts its fast reference: eight banks permit only 19 complete masks
versus the current 43, while sixteen permit 38. These conditional targets make
workload reduction a measurable discriminator. A larger alphabet is not free state storage; require finite mechanically stable
states and executable transitions before timing credit. This changes the premise
that repeated binary row words are the unavoidable workload, rather than merely
speeding an unqualified actuator. Inspect existing multistable/rotary-stop and
support-transfer evidence before choosing embodiments. Keep mechanical, fluidic
and other memories as comparison seeds, not prescribed answers.

This is a research direction, not an accepted architecture or a second campaign
plan in the engineering repository. Future execution belongs in Paperclip, with
bounded synthesis and termination on unresolved state/support topology. CTO
retains technical ownership. The outstanding risks are still complete state
storage, affordable addressing, support transfer, manufacturing lifetime,
readback, assembly and full-machine physical qualification.
