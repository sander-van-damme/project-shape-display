# Retain diverse cross-physics survivors without convergence

**Decision:** conclude the three-family discrimination campaign without selecting
a product architecture. Stop the tested full-field electroadhesive/HV507 route,
the pressure-cell sine grid and magnetic cases with overlapping selection
intervals. Retain guided magnetic selection and rolling/folded pressure concepts
as conditional reserves. None has demonstrated a complete affordable machine.
Prior maturity or number of documents gives no candidate priority.

The common full-field mechanical schedule in E-063/E-066 has <2.3486 s for each
of nine **complete** contact events under its motion/overhead assumptions.
This is a budget, not measured performance. Pressure has no complete <30-s
schedule; magnetic serial row/polarity programming consumes its own event
budget. All must preserve unchanged-region support, quantify shared-frame
motion and recover from failed support proof. Every added local actuator,
contact, conductor, seal or spring is multiplied by 6,400 sites.

No credible complete-system Pareto dominance follows: electroadhesive circuit
cost is decisively disqualifying, while magnetic and pressure costs, timing and
lifetime remain unknown. The latter cannot receive zero cost/risk for missing
evidence. Tight geometry/field scenarios are not calibrated X1C priors. Common
batch, spatial alignment and material errors can invalidate whole modules;
none of the case counts estimates production yield or service reliability.

## Portfolio and next investigation

Retain A-018 microvolume phase-change collars and A-022 structural displacement
layers as distinct reserves. Thermal load-bearing volume and cooling remain
A-018's gates. A-022's offset-invariant drive access is a higher-value next
computational opportunity than another minor tightening of these three contact
models: its stored displacement can replace the long-tail moving grip, but it
must implement selection, load transfer and reset across all upper-stage
offsets. Compare fixed, co-moving and travelling drive access against a simple
rack mechanism before any expensive structural analysis. Reject geometries
that merely encode binary heights without a reachable drive/load path.

Preserve E-063's exclusions: row-serial SMA heating, direct 10-N electroadhesive
support in its tested envelope, taut common membrane and planar 13+ stage
scissor refinement. Different heating schedules, contact area, stored membrane
area or structural topology can reopen them. Temporal capture shares the
support-transfer scheduling problem; it is not an extra actuator principle.
A-023 remains a product-incompatible counterfactual: its smooth hills do not
preserve narrow walls or independent regional motion. No requirement changes.

No fabrication/purchase follows. Further detail on magnetic/pressure reserves
requires materially changed geometry or credible component/process evidence.
Reopening does not require human approval; it requires the stated engineering
evidence. All results are analytical/computational, not hardware qualification.
