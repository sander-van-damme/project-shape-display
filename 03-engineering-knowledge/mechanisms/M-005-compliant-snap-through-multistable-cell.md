**Core principle:** Elastic geometry snaps between stable configurations without conventional
bearings or assembled springs.

**What it solves well:** Potentially eliminates purchased cell-level memory parts.

**What it does not solve:** Shared addressing and long-stroke energy delivery remain external.

**Key risks:** PLA creep/fatigue, temperature, print variation and snap-force spread.

**Cheapest discriminating experiment:** A replicated parameter sweep measuring snap force, stable displacement, creep and
cycle life.
