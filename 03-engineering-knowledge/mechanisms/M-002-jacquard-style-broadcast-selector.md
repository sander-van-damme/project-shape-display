**Core principle:** A pattern medium or selector layer decides which members of a repeated array
respond to one shared motion.

**What it solves well:** Large-scale selection with little local power.

**What it does not solve:** It does not inherently provide dynamic rewriting, 40 mm motion or load support.

**Cheapest discriminating experiment:** A 5×5 selector coupon with adversarial adjacent patterns and one common stroke.
