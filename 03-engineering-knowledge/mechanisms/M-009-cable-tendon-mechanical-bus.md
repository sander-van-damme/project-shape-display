**Core principle:** Remote actuators transmit force through flexible tension members, optionally with
shared or selectable couplers.

**What it solves well:** Moves bulky hardware away from the dense cell region.

**What it does not solve:** Dedicated one-cable-per-cell routing does not scale; selection must itself be
multiplexed.

**Key risks:** Stretch, preload, friction, hysteresis and cable management.

**Cheapest discriminating experiment:** Five outputs on one shared bus with selectable coupling; measure displacement
error and hysteresis under load.
