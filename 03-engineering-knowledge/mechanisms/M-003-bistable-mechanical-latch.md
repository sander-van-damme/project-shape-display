**Core principle:** A mechanism has two stable states and changes only after crossing a mechanical
threshold.

**What it solves well:** Passive state retention and potentially passive load support.

**What it does not solve:** Addressing and programming energy remain external.

**Key risks:** Toggle-force variation, creep, accidental release and manufacturing consistency
at 5.08 mm pitch.

**Cheapest discriminating experiment:** Single-cell and 3×3 coupons measuring toggle force, holding load, repeatability
and cycle wear.
