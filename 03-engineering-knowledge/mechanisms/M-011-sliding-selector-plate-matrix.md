**Core principle:** Thin sliding bars or plates create temporary mechanical paths or constraints.
Orthogonal row/column motion can form coincidence selection.

**What it solves well:** Many intersections can be addressed from a small number of edge actuators.

**What it does not solve:** State retention and power delivery are separate.

**Key risks:** Accumulated friction, plate deflection, combinatorial transactions and clearance.

**Cheapest discriminating experiment:** A 5×5 orthogonal selector matrix plus common lift, including checkerboard and
single-cell patterns.
