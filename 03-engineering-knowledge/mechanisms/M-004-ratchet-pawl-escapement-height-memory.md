**Core principle:** An indexed member moves incrementally while a pawl prevents reverse motion until
release.

**What it solves well:** Discrete position memory, indexing and load support.

**What it does not solve:** Selective addressing and fast scalable reset remain separate problems.

**Cheapest discriminating experiment:** One cell followed by a 1×5 shared-drive row; measure missed/double steps, holding
force, release force and wear.
