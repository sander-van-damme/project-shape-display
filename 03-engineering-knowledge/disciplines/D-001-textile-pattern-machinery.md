Jacquard, dobby, knitting and related machinery solve a recurring problem: many repeated outputs must be selected from shared machine motion.

Transferable families include punched/patterned media, hooks and followers, sliding selector bars, pattern chains, drums and tapes. The key lesson is the separation of **selection** from **power**.

Useful search vocabulary: Jacquard, dobby, heddle selection, needle selection, pattern chain, selector plate and cam box.

The analogy does not solve 40 mm load-bearing terrain travel by itself.
