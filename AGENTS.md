# Instructions for Codex and one-off contributors

This is an **owner-directed project**, not an autonomous laboratory. Do the assigned task; no self-started research campaigns, issue chains, audits, agent handoffs or status-only commits. Require a concrete question, useful deliverable and stop condition. Optimize owner time, tokens and filament.

**Primary starting point: [DES-009](08-integrated-designs/DES-009-traveling-motor-bank-screw-pins/DES-009-traveling-motor-bank-screw-pins.md).** The chosen direction is a **traveling motor bank with compact screw pins**, provisionally 4.4-mm pins / 5.08-mm pitch. Ø8-mm motors do not fit one per adjacent pin in a single straight row; bank packaging is open. Build on this direction unless evidence or an explicit owner decision justifies reconsideration.

Physical starting evidence: [E-301](06-experiments/E-301-pico-w-motorcontroller.md) reports **one microstepper moving one screw pin up and down**. The complete bank, automatic coupling and parallel operation remain physically unproven. Pico W and TMC2208 are the demonstrated test basis, not final electronics for every future design. [E-295](06-experiments/E-295-compact-screw-drive-development.md) supplies compact geometry; [E-302](06-experiments/E-302-improved-microstepper-test-frame.md) supplies revised frame/software, not a new physical pass. [E-297](06-experiments/E-297-two-half-row-column-clutch-print-tests.md) and older DES/experiments remain historical references, not active assignments.

Prioritize simplicity, low cost, limited repeated mechanical complexity and actual D&D playability: **25.4-mm game squares, thin boundary walls and room for miniature bases**. Aim for independent up/down motion without disturbing neighbors. Read the [mission](01-project-description/shape-display-mission.md), [priorities](02-design-criteria/current-prototype-priorities.md) and only relevant criteria for substantial design work; DES-009 updates their earlier open architecture choice. Optical readback, drift correction and elaborate homing/recovery are later work. Preserve basic physical safety.

Distinguish assumptions, calculations, CAD, slicer output, owner observations and measurements. Preserve negative evidence and provenance. Count complete support, force, cost, assembly and repair; simulation is not a physical pass. Stages 03–08: knowledge, candidates, questions, evidence/source, decisions, designs. Historical owner ideas are not a task queue. Keep changes focused; do not rewrite historical experiments for style. Use existing ID conventions and ./repo help/check; no new experiment for documentation edits, mandatory Paperclip process or hard-coded machine paths. Run pertinent checks and report limits.

For explicitly assigned printing, follow owner-print-tests/AGENTS.md: configured X1 Carbon Bambu .3mf projects, tiny tests, no owner deadline. No unrequested purchases, account changes or follow-on tasks. **Stop at the requested deliverable.**
