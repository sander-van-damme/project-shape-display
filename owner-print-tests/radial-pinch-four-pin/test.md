# Printtest — four independent radial pinch outputs

**Status:** READY

**Priority:** P2

**Last reviewed:** 2026-10-14

**Reason:** smallest constructed shared-strip discriminator: four active pins and four physical unchanged neighbors. Existing P1 short-thread fit remains unchanged. READY means files/instructions supplied, not demonstrated fit.

**Technical source:** [DES-007](../../08-integrated-designs/DES-007-radial-pinch-belt-row-head/DES-007-radial-pinch-belt-row-head.md), especially its CAD and verification.txt.

## 1. Why this test? — engineer

**Question:** can one strip independently drive neighboring screw columns in both directions, release them and leave all other columns stationary and supported?

**Why physical:** strip traction, thin-layer durability, printed thread retention and adhesive/socket stiffness cannot be established by Boolean CAD. This is not another tolerance comparison: eight identical nominal thread pairs exercise a complete shared drive. No fit variants multiply this plate; P1 already examines thread fit.

**Decision:** a pass permits force/speed characterization of one bank. It does not qualify full-array speed, 50 mm stroke, motor choice or mass production. Backdrive, unintended rotation, binding, breakage or repeated strip slip/tearing stops this embodiment. Do not fix a failed test by increasing force until parts deform.

## 3. Assembly and short test — engineer

[Printable sections and geometry checks](engineering-drawings.pdf) accompany these instructions. The first two sheets show nominal assembly/selection; later sheets preserve gameplay failures and conditional full-machine timing. They are generated by DES-007/analysis/drawings.py and are not photographs or physical validation.

### Assemble the frame

1. Separate the parts and record any failed/warped features before cleanup. There are six spacers of each height27,12,2.2,12.2,8,7.2 mm, plus two2 mm head spacers. Measure/sort them; do not confuse2.0/2.2. The plate layout is reproducible in `analysis/build.py`.
2. Thread each pin gently onto its screw before closing the frame. If a pair cannot turn by hand without damage, record fit failure and stop. Do not sand/tap individual thread pairs into apparent success. Carefully cut screw brace tabs at the outer collar only. Front four screws have the shorter lower shaft; rear four have6 mm longer shafts. Guide and journal holes are nominal, not guaranteed fits.
3. Stack six tie-bolt stations around the foot. From the foot top upward:27 spacer → lower3 mm split bed →12 spacer → upper3 mm split bed →2.2 spacer →1.6 keeper →12.2 spacer →2 lower guide →8 spacer →2 upper guide →7.2 spacer →2 roof. Each journal bed consists of three pieces; assemble them around the shafts, with .2 mm split gaps. Do not force integral rims through closed bores. The six post coordinates are X−9/28.05 and Y−7/3.175/17 relative to front-left pin(0,0).
4. Collars sit on the upper bed with keeper above. Tighten frame evenly, just enough to locate plates without warping. Thread phase adjusts individually during assembly; do not force all screws to a common phase. Both square guides must allow each pin to move independently. Establish all eight pin tops near50 mm above the lower-bed bottom (q≈3; operating range47…53). Record actual heights. The roof captures excessive upward travel; it must not carry a normal test load.

### Assemble the strip head

5. Mount head on two2 mm spacers above the foot, using its two separate bolts; head bottom is Z−25 when lower-bed bottom is0. Seat five Ø1 separators in head holes, bottomZ−24.8, top−1; adhesive retains them without protruding into moving spaces. They are the positive OFF-span stops, not optional guides.
6. Seat each Ø.8 link in its printed base, with the printed tip on top; link bottom localZ1, top19.8, tip socketZ17.6…19.8. Link center is offset3.175 mm toward the next column from the working pressure face. Align all four pressure faces toward the front-row rims before adhesive cures. Keep tip orientation identical; check clearance to rear rims. Steel links cannot be replaced by printed uprights.
7. Slide bases into separate tracks, fit the cover and form adjustment threads gently with actual M3 screws. Manual return tabs project through cover slots. OFF base coordinateY−4.4; ON approximately−4.0: only .4 mm travel. A screw pushes; it does not pull the shoe back. To disengage, loosen it, then pull that tab rearward. All OFF faces must visibly clear the strip/rims. Screw turns do not measure force; stop at first usable contact.
8. Feed the strip alongX, standing3 mm tall atZ−4…−1, behind the five separators and in front of pressure faces. Keep ample tail both ends. OFF front face isY−2.8, .3 mm clear of the front rim tangent. Apply gentle hand tension; record any curl/tracking problem. Never assume the nominal gap survives print/wire errors.

### Exercise isolation, direction and retention

9. Mark every rim and measure all eight heights at the available instrument resolution. With all shoes OFF, pull the strip both ways: no wheel should turn. Then try alternating masks1010 and0101, slowly in both directions, returning nearq3. Four rear-row pins are always unchanged witnesses. An unintended rim rotation fails isolation even if a height change is too small to measure.
10. Fromq≈3 command front `[+3,−3,+1,0]`: engage0/2, pull5.236 mm; stop, release2; pull another10.472 with0; stop, release0; engage1 and reverse15.708; stop/release1. Expected ideal finalq=[6,0,4,3]. Confirm actual direction with a small first pull and invert the defined strip sign if necessary. Never force an endpoint. Record actual travel/slip, backlash, unchanged neighbors and release behavior. Return by the inverse sequence. No at-speed release.
11. Repeat up to20 gentle cycles, stopping immediately on interference, backdrive, loose bonds or strip damage. Record actual count, not a presumed pass. After release record all eight heights at0,60,600 seconds, initially unloaded. If unloaded motion is sound, an optional stable measured5–20 g load can use the inverted load probe: its Ø3 stem passes through the roof to touch one pin; the Ø10 disk stays above. Confirm visible clearance to roof/other parts throughout; otherwise the roof could falsely carry the load and invalidate retention evidence. Record mass and duration; do not extrapolate to a miniature rating or use a large load.

**Initial success:** all four selected outputs move in both directions, distinct commanded travel is recognizable, every unselected rim stays stationary at the observation resolution, pins retain height after stopped release, and no loss of thread engagement or damage occurs. Record any positional error; first-prototype drift is not silently converted to accuracy qualification. A pin that drops/backdrives or a neighbor that turns fails this embodiment.

**Unknown:** unprinted parts, unmeasured forces, unavailable instruments, uncertain roof contact or absent results remain unknown. Twenty cycles do not establish service life or thousands-part yield.
