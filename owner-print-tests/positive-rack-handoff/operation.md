# Operating sequence — positive-rack article

Complete [assembly.md](assembly.md) and use the stop conditions in [test.md](test.md).

## One complete signed transaction

**Only heights 0, 5 and 10 mm and single 5-mm changes are allowed.** No 15-mm terrain height and no ±10-mm pulse belong to this revised article. At10 mm plus the nominal0.3-mm unloading motion, the shaft bottom has only0.7 mm insertion reserve below the bottom guide. Avoid extra overtravel.

1. Select an arbitrary subset. Remove its dog keepers, slide those handles forward to ON, and insert their keepers into the now-empty rear ends. Leave every other handle locked OFF. Each working tooth must enter its own rear groove; do not push the pin sideways to achieve entry.
2. Hold the fixed frame down. Raise the common carriage gently until selected pins rise slightly and their ground-pawl contacts are visibly unloaded. The nominal 0.6-mm carriage rise includes 0.3-mm dog clearance and 0.3-mm pin lift, but printed play means **this is not a guaranteed actuator stroke**.
3. Retract only the selected ground pawls. Hook a ground keeper over the tab with its peg above the fixed front wall; pull it forward until the peg aligns with its hole and lower it. About 1.4-mm lift clears the wall while retaining tab engagement. The hole/slot clearances can reduce nominal 1.05-mm retraction by up to0.40 mm: inspect that every nose actually clears its rack. The other pawls stay on their original ledges throughout.
4. Move the common carriage in the chosen direction until **every selected target groove can accept its ground pawl**, keeping the head approximately level. Do not wait for every cap to reach its final seated height before restoring support. A 3.20-mm groove and 1.20-mm pawl give a nominal 2.00-mm insertion window. During a downward change, gravity-following and deliberately dragged pins can sit at different positions within that window: approximately target+0.30 mm and target+1.15 mm in the illustrated ideal case. The difference includes **0.60-mm groove backlash plus up to 0.25-mm key-slot travel**. Do not precompensate the entire head by 0.85 mm; that could push an already lower pin past its intended support. Observe each target groove.
5. While the dogs still capture the selected pins, lift/remove each ground keeper and let that pawl return into the target groove. Confirm its foot remains on the ledge and its nose fully enters. Do not withdraw a dog while the pin lacks ground support.
6. With **all selected ground pawls restored**, seat the pins in short, observed movements. Remove deliberate drag. As soon as one pin rests on its ground pawl, hold the head steady, adjust only within that dog's available clearance, remove its keeper, and slide that dog OFF when free; replace its keeper in the front pocket. Retire completed dogs individually before any further shared motion could force them against a seated pawl. Keep the other pins captured while resolving their remaining gap. If a seated dog cannot be withdrawn freely, stop; do not drive the common head farther. Final heights are established by the ground supports after this sequence, not by one blind common-head distance.
7. Return the empty carriage to the mark. Recheck all active and witness heights against the same fixed reference, and check the reference/frame itself did not move.
