# Assembly instructions — positive-rack article

Read the printing/hardware checks in [test.md](test.md) first. Source and hardware are the same revision.

## Assemble without trapping components

Front is the side with the pawl pull tabs, the fixed keeper wall and the four drive-key handles. Racks have their grooved faces facing front/back. Keep pins and their small caps out of the assembly until step 7.

1. **Build the moving carriage.** Put four drive keys in the base, with teeth pointing toward the pin row and handles at the front. They must slide between the two hard stops without flexing. Fit the lid over the rear key bearings and around the two tall bosses; secure its two M3×16 screws and nuts gently. Move each handle backward to OFF and insert a dog keeper into the empty front end of its pocket. Remove that keeper before changing its handle. Do not drive through a keeper.
2. **Prepare each comb and clamp while free of the frame.** Lower the matching root clamp over a free comb from above. Its cap teeth pass between the four pawl heads; its front backbone stays in front of the leaves. Its narrow lower block sits immediately in front of the comb root. The cap teeth rest over the root's inter-leaf portions. Preinsert two M3×10 screws through each clamp; their heads sit on top. Do not yet attach nuts. Stop if any spring must be bent sideways to force the clamp past it.
3. **Thread the two comb/clamp units into the seat — witness row first.** Hold the seat separately. Start the witness unit below it and approximately **2.15 mm toward the front** of its final position. Its four heads pass upward through the widened rear leaf slots, each slot 3.30 mm front/back. Raise the head undersides at least 0.3–0.6 mm above the seat's upper surface, slide the unit backward 2.15 mm to its working position, then lower the heads onto their four ledges. The common root and clamp remain below the seat throughout. Repeat for the active/front unit. Keep both units supported; their loose roots must not twist. This order avoids the adjacent root-clamp ears crossing during insertion.
4. **Complete the upper fixed subassembly.** Put four 16-mm spacers on the lower guide, then lower the seat/comb/clamp bundle onto them. Both comb roots enter the lower guide's shallow pockets; all four preinserted root screws enter their holes. Add the four nuts under the lower guide. Tighten gently through the seat's four 3.5-mm Allen-access holes while holding the nuts. The defined contact pads must clamp the comb root before the bolt flanges or narrow root-block underside touch the fixed frame; those other faces have 0.30-mm relief. Tighten only until each root is captured and cannot lift/rock when one pawl tab is pulled. A small remaining flange gap is intentional. Do not apply normal structural-bolt torque or try to close this gap; M3 screws can readily crush the small PLA contact pads. If the flanges bottom while the root is still loose, stop and record the fit failure; do not hide it with extra torque. Add four 7-mm spacers and the upper guide, front access cutout facing the active row. Its matching tool holes allow later root-clamp retightening.
5. **Add the bottom frame and carriage.** Insert four M3×80 frame screws from the upper guide through the entire upper stack. Support/turn this upper subassembly with the screw heads down so the four long free shanks point upward. Slide four 38-mm sleeves over them. Feed the assembled carriage over the two front sleeves from their open ends; it must move freely. Fit the bottom guide over all four bolts, with its raised bearing region facing into the mechanism. Add four nuts. Tighten opposite corners gradually, checking that neither the frame nor sleeves become bowed and that the carriage still slides. The nominal clamped stack is 76 mm; an 80-mm screw leaves 1.6 mm beyond a 2.4-mm nut. Do not crush printed spacers to hide a mismatched length.
6. **Stand the rig on its four cups.** Turn upright and set the four exposed bottom nuts/tips into the foot-cup recesses. Their annular tops carry the bottom guide. The cups are gravity supports, not tension fasteners: keep one hand on the fixed frame during carriage motion and do not lift the machine by its carriage. A common bench clamp may be used if already available; record it. The bench is nominal Z−31 and the lowest pin tip is Z−25, giving 6-mm clearance.
7. **Insert witness pins first, then active pins, from the top.** Retract the appropriate ground-pawl tab before its shaft passes; never force a shaft through an engaged pawl. Insert through upper, seat, lower and bottom guides. Because the rear row is not a selectable output, use a fingertip or the blunt end of the Allen key to hold its tab clear while lowering that pin. The front row is still empty, giving access. Release the tab and seat each pin gently on the selected groove. Repeat the active row; its keeper can hold a released pawl during insertion. Caps cannot pass the 5×3-mm guide holes. All dogs must remain OFF during pin installation.
8. **Set initial heights and a carriage mark.** Begin with all eight pins seated at height 5 mm. Their cap tops should be 15 mm above the upper guide's top; heights 0/5/10 correspond to cap tops 10/15/20 mm above it. Measure relative to the fixed frame and record resolution. Gently position the empty carriage so each dog can enter a groove without pushing a pin. Choose the station near the illustrated home pose (bearing-boss tops approximately8.9 mm below the lower guide underside), then mark the sleeve at the base edge. This mark aids return; actual clearance and engagement must still be observed.
