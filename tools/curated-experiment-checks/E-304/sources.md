The E-303 screw model is imported directly, without editing its source or promoting its assumptions into measurements. Its 90° V-thread approximation uses mean diameter 2.3 mm and lead 3 mm; guide opposition and parasitic torque remain assumptions. The selected CAD reuses the actual E-295 retained screw-thread and pin meshes from E-297, preserving their detailed thread profile instead of substituting a nominal cylinder. Only the support/drive end of the new screw changes.
