What measurable disturbance is acceptable during a fog-of-war reveal? The
E-009 screening threshold is 0.10 mm peak and residual motion of an untouched
loaded neighbour, but this is not yet an approved product limit or a hardware
result.

Test: representative miniature on a loaded untouched tile; reset/rewrite adjacent region. Measure peak vertical/lateral movement, residual height error, miniature displacement/tipping and update time. Sweep 5×5, 10×10 and 20×20 boundaries before choosing tile size. Use the E-009 protocol and ADR-004 disposition; assumed frame stiffness cannot resolve this question.
