Can shared hardware load hundreds of independent choices without hundreds of purchased actuators?

Test: final-pitch 2×4 bank-addressed register/gate strip. Measure loaded complete dwell, toggle force, leakage, addressing time and reset. Geometry/dwell calculations alone do not establish reliable selection. Any full-scale extrapolation must count state-loading, carriage travel, drive force and retries.
