# Q-010: complete arbitrary-map programming boundary

What complete physical information/programming path fits purchased cost and sustained arbitrary-map timing across materially different architecture families?

Count generation of the requested state, local memory/selection, addressing, transport/clamping, registration, power transfer, readback, recovery and reusable/consumed media. Prepared patterns or buffering do not remove the cost and latency of creating an unseen map. Include both visible transition and sustained map-to-map time.

E-017/A-011 provide one historical four-plane comparison, not a uniquely necessary solution. Its 20.18 s estimate depends on eight addressed positions and four simultaneous plane writes with inherited rates; the mechanism remains undefined. E-044/E-046 retain cost and compatibility limitations. ADR-006 preserves boundary accounting; ADR-009 opens the architecture search.

Resolve computationally by comparing concrete complete mechanisms and schedules under manufacturing/actuation uncertainty. Identify selection leakage, arbitrary state reach, regional isolation and fault recovery. Use physical calibration only when it changes a decision. No existing coupon or four-plane representation is mandatory.
