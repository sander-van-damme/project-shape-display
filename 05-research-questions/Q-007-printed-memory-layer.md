Can a thin multistable printed layer retain independently writable state with tolerable cross-talk and variation?

Generate and screen state-retaining geometries for pitch, write-force window, neighbour coupling and retention under bounded manufacturing/material variability. Compare binary, unary and other complete decoders. Use targeted calibration for creep/friction or cycle life only when uncertainty changes selection. Use a separate mechanism for 40 mm travel and service load; quantify writer throughput.
