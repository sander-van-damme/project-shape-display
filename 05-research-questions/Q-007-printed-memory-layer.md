Can a thin multistable printed layer retain independently writable state with tolerable cross-talk and variation?

Test final-pitch coupons: minimum reliable pitch, write-force window, neighbor coupling, retention/creep, cycle life and batch variability. Compare binary versus four unary layers. Use a separate mechanism for 40 mm travel and service load; quantify writer throughput.
