# Reader dwell does not make a reversible cam retain terrain

**Stop the direct slotted-bridge/reversible-cam package.** Independent reader
dwell resolves E-092's query/reseat interference within the tested bound. A
separate two-position flag can also retain authorization geometrically. Neither
makes the support outputs persist after the energy cam resets. The generated
captured cam either retracts the ground dog, reacquires a deposited column, or
jams against a retained output. Its slotted transmission additionally consumes
more lateral clearance than E-092's 0.7-mm dog stroke permits.

These are scoped failures of generated coupling sections, not an impossibility
of multilevel memory. A disengaging drive plus positively retained support, an
over-center output, or a different stateful cam could escape the reset failure;
none is generated or accepted here. End this finite-package investigation at
that decision gate and compare the accumulated package burden with the selective
deck and direct writer before commissioning another local mechanism. Do not
keep granting each missing latch, clutch and proof as a free interface.

Input main `90322c1`. Reproduce with
`python3 tools/curated-experiment-checks/E-093/cam_coupling.py`.
Evidence: deterministic planar contact, bounded kinematics and quasistatic
support replay; self-review only. No fabricated parts, measured priors,
probabilistic yield, force sizing or hardware qualification. All geometry is in
mm. This compares selector persistence and transmission principles within the
retained-command family; it is not a new complete architecture.

## Two ways to preserve selection

**Sampled flag:** a separate translating shutter stores 0 or 1.5 mm. It has two
1.3±0.1-mm square-key holes with a positive intervening land, read by a
0.6±0.1-mm key. Setter error ±0.2 leaves 0.05-mm insertion reserve. The shutter's
output tongue occupies y=[−2−s,0.8−s]; the output lug spans y=[0,1]. With the
key engaged, ±0.45-mm free play, an additional ±0.1-mm tongue datum and ±0.1-mm
tongue-edge error leave 0.05-mm minimum selected clearance and 0.15-mm minimum
unselected blocking overlap. These errors can align across an entire bank.

The finite check includes both holes, the land, all retained error corners,
blocked engaged transitions and the whole withdrawn-key sweep. Equality and
sign authorize a separate compliant common SET pusher; their probes must not
supply column-support energy. Required order is: inhibit support cam, unlock
flag, set through authorization, lock and prove flag, withdraw query, transfer,
prove grounded state, unlock/reset/relock flag, prove neutral. Actual pusher,
key keeper/drive, probe-to-pusher assembly and individual proof are **unproved
interfaces**, so this is a retention section, not a passed sampler. A retained
SET bit after failed reset admits an unwanted subsequent transfer. At least
12,800 more local sliders (flag and key) raise E-092's count to **57,600**,
before support retention, transmission bridges, guides and readback.

**Independent reader dwell:** mount the query carriage on a separate bank
coordinate r. At group k, hold r=5k while the elevator moves from 5k+2.2 to 5k.
The query probe may remain inserted through that reseat because its slot-relative
position is fixed. Withdraw and prove the probe clear before moving r to the
next group; hold command memory unchanged throughout. E-091's finite sign and
RETURN gates remain necessary. This needs another controlled shared coordinate
per bank, with force/holding/registration and its own position proof unqualified.
It avoids the extra per-cell sampled flag/key; it does not hold the support dogs.

For a parasitic coupling ratio lambda, the reader shifts by at most
`2.2*1.01*lambda`. Retain E-091's ±0.65-mm datum, ±1% gain and slot/pin width
bounds. Finite polygons check every same-sign command/query pair at all corners
and three reseat positions. Lambda=0,0.25,0.5 preserve intended entry and exclude
wrong entries; 0.75 and 1 each produce both target misses and wrong entries.
The conservative sufficient bound is **lambda <0.60756**, from the existing
1.35-mm reserve. Piecewise-affine relative motion makes endpoint bounds cover
the continuous inserted stroke; the midpoint is a holdout. These lambda values
are competing uncertainty scenarios, not measured coupling or probability.
A separate coordinate therefore removes the kinematic contradiction, conditional
on an implementation meeting the bound; an assumed independent axis is not a
priced or qualified drive.

## A finite bridge adds consequential lost motion

A reader-borne drive pin cannot directly move a grounded dog across the signed
80-mm reader travel. Generate a bridge attached to that dog, with a vertical
slot spanning −41…41 mm. A square 0.6±0.1-mm drive pin, ±0.2-mm vertical datum
and ±0.1-mm slot-end placement have **0.35-mm minimum end clearance** across
r=−40…40. The 82-mm slot is a separate planar lane; its rail thickness, out-of-plane
stiffness, 6,400-fold material, mounting and 3D packing are not established.

Use lateral slot width 1.3±0.1 mm, pin width 0.6±0.1 and bridge-to-dog datum
±0.2. With no maintained preload, lateral error can reach **±0.65 mm**. The
cam may reverse its loaded flank during reset; a one-direction offset correction
does not remove both extrema. This is a new coupling stack, distinct from
E-091's memory error, and is not automatically covered by it.

E-092's nominal withdrawn dog tip is x=−0.2, and its inserted tip is x=0.5.
The bridge permits x=+0.45 while nominally withdrawn, or x=−0.15 while nominally
inserted. Generated rack rectangles show collision with a land during a
subsequent 5-mm move; the other extreme has no bearing overlap. A cam endpoint
reading would accept either fault. Just providing 0.05-mm reserves on both
sides would require at least **1.4-mm stroke**, versus 0.7 mm, before dog-size,
flexure, wear or angular errors. This lower bound only guarantees positive
geometric overlap, not adequate bearing strength. Positive preload/different
transmission or more stroke would change this scoped failure, but none repairs
the separate reset contradiction below. Do not optimize this bridge first.

## Captured cam: safe transfer, unsafe return

Grant perfect selection and nominal lateral transmission here, to isolate reset
from their earlier failures. Generate two finite parallel rails enclosing a
0.4-mm square pad. Their centerline is y=0.175x; a 4-mm axial carriage stroke
produces the 0.7-mm lateral dog stroke. Vertical clearance is 0.05 mm per side,
rails are 0.4 mm thick, and their finite x extent is −1…5 mm. Polygon sweeps
at 1/16/64 subdivisions verify free forward/reverse motion and contact beyond
each rail. Constant slope makes the analytical envelope continuous between
samples. Separate cam lanes drive ground and elevator dogs; their physical
selection assembly is granted for this failure bound, not credited as built.

At full stroke ground is inserted. E-092's 2.2→0 reseat transfers load onto
it; withdrawing the elevator dog then completes deposition. In the frictionless model, returning the
captured ground cam brings its output back: nominal bearing overlap disappears
at carriage coordinate x=8/7 mm and is absent at home even allowing the groove
clearance. Three attempted reset policies fail:

1. Reset ground with grip withdrawn: neither dog supports the column.
2. Reinsert grip at e=0 before ground reset: the grip fits the finite pocket but
   lies below its ceiling. Over all 9,216 old/target/error cases, withdrawing
   ground permits **0.6–1.8 mm fall to static equilibrium**, including target
   tooth error and elastic deflection. Both possible low-coordinate reset
   orders fail; this is not a simulated impact trajectory.
3. Lift until the reinserted grip actually supports load, then reset ground:
   this safely restores grip-only carriage, undoing deposition. The next 5-mm
   macro move moves the already deposited column by 5 mm.

Trying to hold the ground dog with a retained stop while returning the still
captured cam instead jams. At home, even 0.1-mm groove play cannot accommodate
the ≥0.25-mm output shift needed for 0.05-mm bearing overlap; finite polygons
intersect for clearance scenarios 0,0.05,0.1. A successful flag hold is therefore
not a clutch. Unloading and resetting the selector cannot disconnect the
energy transmission by itself.

Loaded lateral withdrawal may jam before the predicted drop if friction or
deformation prevents it; no extraction force is credited. That does not provide
a successful reset.

A local history requirement makes the scope clear: at the next query station,
a previously deposited cell and a still-carried cell can both have inactive
equality yet require opposite dog states (commands +5 and +15 mm at a +10-mm query
provide a finite same-sign witness). A single-valued linkage to that
instantaneous query and a reset cam cannot supply both histories. The command
word contains enough information for a different inequality decoder, but the
implemented equality gate does not. Retained dog states or a different decoder/
cam principle remain necessary; a separate redundant “done” bit is not proven
necessary, and E-089's common RETURN-inhibit result is unchanged.

## Decision and evidence limits

Prefer independent dwell over the sampled-flag subsection for any future
retained-command coupling investigation: it exchanges 12,800 extra sliders for
one unqualified reader coordinate per bank. This is a conditional complexity
choice, not a Pareto winner or machine acceptance. The 82-mm transmission lane,
E-091's 164-mm memory sweep, E-092's **199.6-mm adverse elevator travel**, extra
18 motion legs and at least **44,800** local sliders remain in its boundary;
bridges, persistent dog locks and disengaging drives add to that floor.

Stop the generated bridge/captured reversible-cam embodiment. Reopening requires
a changed transmission that maintains lateral engagement and a finite energy
path that resets while preserving ground support; better equality sensing alone
cannot do it. Move to the campaign's complete-system comparison with this
scoped exclusion, the surviving subsections and the unproved interfaces made
explicit. A further local redesign must first earn value against the selective
deck and direct-positioning controls. No printing follows from this result.

Manufacturing bounds are epistemic boxes allowing coherent bank errors, not
independent-cell yield estimates. Rail deformation, dog strength, friction,
creep, wear, dynamic force, actual proof/readback and false acceptance remain
unmodelled. The reset failure already exists at nominal rigid geometry; those
unknowns do not rescue the stated direct coupling. Self-review checks the
limiting cases, exact overlap/clearance bounds, finite polygons and E-092's
support paths; it is not independent experimental validation.
