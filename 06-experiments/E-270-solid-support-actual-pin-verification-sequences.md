# Actual-pin readback above solid supports: access, coverage and shared fields

**Retain post-undock top sensing as a conditional architecture comparator.** Local narrow stereo reaches deep
empty wells at both pitches. A shared camera pair can instead cover16 coarse cells with a tall mounting; its
image quality/calibration is unqualified. Contact probing is a slow diagnostic. None verifies a pin hidden
beneath an opaque miniature, nor proves later retention. No sensor, head, print or purchase is selected.

Reproduce with `python3 tools/curated-experiment-checks/E-270/verification.py --check` and `python3
tools/curated-experiment-checks/E-270/shared_field.py --check`; omit flags for tables. Python plus inherited
numpy. **Evidence:** analytical bounds, ray/box calculations, synthetic triangulation, fault witnesses and
conditional schedules; self-review, not physical validation. No safe RPM, force, process yield, optical
accuracy or loaded-neighbor isolation is established.

## Shared camera field: fewer optical paths at the cost of height and calibration

The shared-field triangulator receives supplied correspondences, not rendered images. Covered-pin and
isolation limitations remain.

Two parallel downward-looking cameras have baseline b, height D above the lowest cap, and one common field
covering k adjacent cells. W=(k−1)p is the center-to-center field width. Keeping the previous .4-mm target
patch, ±.2-mm placement/face bounds, and pupil diameter a, a sufficient nearest-neighbor margin is

`m = p − 2.2 − 2e − ((W+b)/2+a/2) S/D − .2(1−S/D)`.

Low edge cells give the worst ray angle; increasing target height reduces the obstruction interval. Both
cameras must fit the entire field even at the highest caps. With an **assumed 1920-pixel horizontal image**,
reserve 10% of its width and choose focal length in pixels `f = .9×1920×(D−S)/(W+b+.4+2e)`. No particular
sensor or lens is implied. At the lowest cap, disparity d=fb/D. A bounded .1-pixel disparity error gives exact
worst depth error `D×.1/(d−.1)`, with calibration otherwise perfect. These are assumed error bounds.

**Finite pupil and image formation cannot be separated.** The manufacturer explanation of [diffraction and the
Airy
disk](https://www.edmundoptics.com/knowledge-center/application-notes/imaging/limitations-on-resolution-and-contrast-the-airy-disk/)
(accessed 2026-10-13) gives ideal Airy diameter 2.44λ(f/#). Expressed in object space for this paraxial model,
the first-dark-ring radius is approximately 1.22λD/a. At assumed 550-nm light, D1000/a.5 gives **1.342 mm**,
much larger than the .2-mm patch half-width. Thus geometric clearance alone does not earn the assigned
.1-pixel disparity bound. Increasing the shared entrance pupil to **3.4 mm** reduces this radius to **.197
mm** while coarse k16/S50 clearance remains **.0938 mm**. This is an ideal diffraction/access comparison, not
a resolved texture, focus, aberration, contrast or subpixel-accuracy pass; centroid accuracy is not identical
to Airy radius. The patch spans only4.83 pixels in that field. Defocus across 50-mm depth and all real image
errors remain unmodeled. Do not refine this small clearance into an optical hardware commitment.

Registration is equally consequential: a .1% error in calibrated fb creates1 mm depth error at D1000; a small
relative camera yaw of10 microradians contributes approximately .25 mm for b40 (D²δθ/b). Neither is a measured
disturbance. A frame reference and calibration must bound these errors after carrier moves; counting pixels
cannot substitute for that evidence.

Synthetic projection/reconstruction returns assigned heights exactly and bounds12,096 noisy coordinate cases.
A wrong neighboring match in a D300/b40/p6.35 example instead returns an apparently in-range **41.10-mm
height** from a flat scene. Its inferred lateral coordinate is off-grid, so a correct geometry gate can reject
this particular error. A whole-pitch error in camera-to-grid registration preserves lattice consistency while
shifting all identities; lattice gating alone cannot reject it. Repeated blank caps supply no demonstrated
correspondence. Require fresh post-release cap evidence, independent board registration, metric calibration,
and explicit occlusion/correspondence confidence; missing evidence means unknown. A projected pattern is a
possible future shared aid, not an implemented decoder or free extra illumination. Miniature-covered cells
remain unknown even with perfect images.

parts, and a larger upper carrier. In the coarse S50 case its pupil plane sits **950 mm above the highest
terrain**. Arms, cables, lighting, transport and player access are real tradeoffs. k4/k8 permit lower stations
