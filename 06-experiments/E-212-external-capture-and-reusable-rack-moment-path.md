## A constructed guide solves a force problem and exposes a different collision

The original web is .8 mm wide, equal to tooth width. A front guide spanning
that width intersects a tooth during height change. Widen the web to 1.6 mm,
leaving the teeth .8 mm wide. Each of two guide stations at z=8/20 mm has finite
front pads outside x=±.5, side walls and a rear wall; a rear spine connects both
stations. The guide envelope is 2.6 mm wide. These are **H reusable guides**, not
6,400 new stationary rack bearings; the wider web still repeats 6,400 times.

A rigid guide entering from +y cannot pass its front pads through the web.
Splitting both stations and spine at x=0 creates two connected halves. Open
each .9 mm, insert 2 mm along y, then close to the clearance setting; reverse
for departure. These nominal paths clear the rack and stationary neighboring
geometry at all three subdivisions. Open width is 4.4 mm. They require two
guided lateral outputs and an insertion axis; no hinge, actuator or synchronizer
is hidden in the geometry. This is useful reusable access construction.

At .15-mm nominal guide clearance, eight coherent corners combine x/y rack
offset ±.05 and web half-width error ±.05 mm. All 520 height samples at the
finest grid clear. More strongly, the eight full 40-mm vertical swept solids
clear; this covers continuous prescribed translation for these corners. It
does not cover rack tilt under guide load. The front pad overlaps each actual
shoulder by at least .20 mm in the tested corners.

Opposed side and front/back reactions at two elevations have rank five in
(Fx,Fy,Mx,My,Mz); z translation stays free without friction. Nonnegative signed
unit-wrench reconstruction residual is <1e−14. This is the **contacting limit**,

jaw reactions: guide-supported vertical reactions could be 10/0 N for this

before actual pressure distribution. No material allowable is inferred.

Passive clearance permits approximately .30/12=.025 rad pitch take-up and

first-order centered-clearance scenarios, not solved loaded poses or a new

Closure, preload, stiffness, readback and yaw load sharing remain owed.
