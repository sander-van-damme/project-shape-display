# Fewer command writes do not yet buy a simpler complete machine

**Park the tested equality-decoded displacement package. Investigate selective
load-bearing height memory next, with the direct writer as control.** The
multilevel write reduction is real in the controller, but the tested finite
coupling fails support retention and already repeats at least 44,800 sliders.
The complete-machine allocation below gives a changed implementation measurable
reopening targets. It does not prove the principle uneconomic or select a
product. The feasible-machine Pareto set remains empty.

Original input main `aec242d`; finite deck itinerary revised from E-095 at
`5b6151a`. Reproduce with
`python3 tools/curated-experiment-checks/E-094/system_budget.py`.
Evidence: deterministic schedule/budget calculation using E-089/E-083 source,
explicit uncertainty scenarios and self-review; no new contact model, sourced
prices, measured manufacturing priors, hardware timing or independent validation.
Generated JSON is disposable. E-091's failed 41-command lock and E-093's failed
bridge/cam remain hard exclusions; a positive timing allocation cannot override
them. Only the nine-level, 17-command section is carried into these budgets.

## Common boundary and different operations

Nine levels span 40 mm on 80×80 cells. The retained/deck/binary full workload
places every signed displacement in every row, as generated by E-089. Direct
positioning separately takes the worst of all nine-level old/new pairs at each
row: the all-displacements map alone omits its slower three-leg transitions.
Local workload is E-089's initially flat 5×5 patch, tested at every start row
0…75; unchanged heads remain idle. An unchanged map requires no mechanism work.
These are stress workloads, not an exhaustive worst-case schedule proof.

B={1,2,4,8,16} fixed independent banks each have 80 data/head coordinates and
80/B rows. The table is a conditional common allocation, not a borrowing of
E-088's rotary writer or E-083's fixed six-second support reserve:

- Rest-to-rest motion uses `2 sqrt(d/a)` below `d=v²/a`, otherwise `d/v+v/a`.
  Central v=200 mm/s, a=5,000 mm/s²; alternatives are 80/1,000 and 400/20,000.
  These inherit E-050's scenario values, not physical capabilities. Speed,
  mass, jerk, flexible dynamics, motor power and bank loads need qualification.
- Retained memory pays E-092's **199.6 mm elevator path**, including take-up and
  each reseat, plus **160 mm separate reader travel**. Reader and elevator
  motions are serialized; no speculative overlap credit. Setting scans forward,
  clearing backward and returning to bank-row-zero home charge two complete
  writer traversals. Lift/engage/withdraw belongs inside each write slot.
- Binary pays the same finite elevator path **counterfactually**, plus E-083's
  force-limited row-cycle source at drag=.005 N, k=.5 N/mm, mass=20 g and
  requested a=20 m/s². Its separate 4-ms row-proof allocation is assumed.
  This does not establish compatibility with the failed cam/support package.
- Selective deck now pays E-095's **94-mm nominal finite pickup/deposition
  path**, home −2 → top45 → home−2, including stopped proof waypoints at each
  old/new stop height +1 mm. The fixed top remains 45 mm for local work. Hooks
  are written forward, unloaded stops backward, hooks cleared forward, then the
  writer returns home: four traversals. Open pickup separates passively, but
  real hook retention, stop setting, readback and error-dependent contact
  microtravel remain unproved; the latter must fit the support slots. Finite
  contact geometry does not qualify a shared hook/stop writer.
- Direct heads pay home→old→new→home, each visited row index and final carriage
  return. Bank-row-zero is the declared parked coordinate; arbitrary parking
  adds dispatch time. Complete head cost includes grip/release/sensing, not
  just one position motor. This changes E-050's itinerary, not its old result.

Central unknown-operation allocations: multilevel write/clear **0.5 s/round**;
hook write/clear **0.1 s/round**; unloaded stop setting **0.5 s/round**; support
transfer **0.1 s/group**, including selection, real load proof, withdrawal,
retention, reset and local settling beyond the charged axial motion. One group
may require many observations; these slots assume an unproved parallel reader.
Preparation, registration, shared interlock proofs and final settling receive
**2 s/map** (also test 6 s). No preparation or clear is hidden outside the cycle.

Charge one detected row retry and one support retry: the former repeats its
complete write slot; the latter adds 2.2-mm up/down and a transfer slot. Direct
heads instead repeat one full worst head route with both support transfers.
These are bounded recovery grants, not fault-frequency estimates. A jam,
reacquisition, long diagnosis, common error or persistent fault stops supported
operation and is outside a successfully completed update. Actual recovery must
prove that it preserves load before this timing can be credited.

## Sensitivity and conditional Pareto limits

48 combinations cross word/stop-setting slots .05/.2/.5/.9 s, transfer slots
.05/.2/.5 s, overhead 2/6 s and zero/one retries. Hook slots remain .1 s. Examples
at 2-s overhead and one retry give minimum passing **tested** bank counts:

| Word / transfer s | Retained | Binary | Deck | Direct |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| .05 / .05 | 1 | 8 | 2 | 4 |
| .20 / .20 | 4 | 8 | 4 | 4 |
| .50 / .50 | 8 | 16 | 8 | 8 |
| .90 / .20 | 8 | 8 | 8 | 4 |

Thus deck is not universally faster, nor is memory necessarily uneconomic.
At B=4, slow/central/fast motion gives retained **38.489/30.623/27.511 s** and
deck **32.156/24.552/21.476 s**. Binary's own force-limited writer is held fixed
in this sensitivity; it is not granted the other axes' acceleration.

The code also computes time/cost frontiers with explicitly hypothetical complete
channel prices varied independently by family, and $0/.02 cell purchases.
These are conditional offers, not feasible machines: low-priced memory can
exchange money for time against deck; cheap direct heads can dominate a deck
on these two axes. Reliability, print/assembly and volume cannot be filled with
invented comparable numbers to manufacture a whole-product Pareto winner.
Unresolved or failed mechanical interfaces exclude every offer from an accepted
machine set.

At B=8, across all 76 patch positions, central local times range
**8.163–10.393 s retained**, **5.902–7.388 binary**, **7.389–9.249 deck** and
**4.556–6.061 direct**. Binary can beat retained memory on this sparse workload
while losing on full-map time. Dispatch, fixed bank alignment, word reset and
home return explain why full-board parallelism does not apply directly.
No physical unchanged-region displacement is bounded. Deck intentionally lifts
changed columns above their final terrain; unchanged hooks must stay out.

## Repeated burden, volume, proof and service

| Family | Repeated structure and unresolved risk |
|---|---|
| Retained dwell | ≥44,800 local sliders: cursor/key, sign shutter/two probes, two support dogs; 108,800 lock positions at H=9; additional support retention, bridges and disengagement still required |
| Sampled-flag alternative | ≥57,600 sliders; 12,800 added flag/key members do not solve cam reset; no advantage demonstrated |
| Binary | 6,400 command dogs and captive keys/feet, two persistent output functions/cell, 80 rails and 80B data bars; 32.512 m active bar length before ends/supports; retention/drive package unresolved |
| Deck | 6,400 load-bearing multilevel stops and selective pickup hooks, local state retention/guides; complete hook isolation, writer torque and service-load support unproved; cannot inherit coupon sheet stiffness |
| Direct | 6,400 racks/pawls/returns and ≥19,200 guide/latch/grip interfaces from A-013, plus 80B complete heads; cost, reach and packing unproved |

Do not equate a slider, detent position, interface or complete drive channel.
The script reports slider-only volume/labor sensitivity, not sliced CAD. At
assumed mean 20 mm³ per member, 44,800 members alone occupy **0.896 litres**;
at assumed 5–15 mm³/s deposition this is **16.6–49.8 hours**, before columns,
rails, supports, waste or reprints. Mean 2/10/30 s insertion/inspection per
member implies **24.9/124.4/373.3 hours**. These deliberately broad hypotheses
can change with integrated manufacture, assembly automation or different geometry;
no actual process time is inferred. Code also tests 5/80 mm³ and 57,600 members.

The 164-mm memory swept extent and 82-mm bridge lane are separate section
reservations, not proven additive board height. Full 3D coexistence, module seams,
X1C build layout, accessible replaceable memory/support cartridges and wiring
remain missing. Deck's 45-mm top excursion needs 5 mm beyond usable height;
retained support needs E-092's additional headroom and signed sweep. Direct
heads need the entire tail stroke plus safe withdrawal. No compactness winner.

Full-map observation **floors** are 25,600 retained (word/clear + support),
32,000 deck (hooks/set/clear + support), 134,400 H=9 binary (complete row states
+ support), 12,800 direct (support). Withdrawal, sign/RETURN gates, support
retention and global readback can add many more. Reducing proofs is not reducing
unsafe-accept probability. Given genuine unconditional per-opportunity bounds,
`P(any unsafe acceptance) ≤ min(1, Σ N_j p_j)` needs no independence. None of
those bounds is known. A common registration bias can corrupt a whole bank;
repeated reads with the same bias do not earn independent retry credit.
E-091/E-092/E-093's error boxes permit coherent errors; their surviving corners
are not manufacturing yield. Wear, friction, creep, layer weakness, dynamic
force and repair success remain unqualified.

## Decision and checks

Stop further equality-latch/cam parameter refinement. Reopening requires finite
reset/disengagement with retained support, robust setting/reading at the intended
resolution, and complete channel/operation budgets satisfying a recomputed
whole-machine allocation. Improved slot entry alone is insufficient. Retain
memory reduction as a useful principle and retain failed source/witnesses.

Selective load-bearing stops earn the next bounded investigation because they
may merge terrain state storage and load support, avoiding this package's
separate command comparator, reader coordinate and two retained output functions.
This is an exploration priority, not demonstrated simplicity: finite selective
pickup and independently supported unchanged columns must be shown first.
Compare rotary stops with materially changed load/support mechanisms and the
direct-head control; do not repeat the old rotor coupon or default to printing.

Self-review checks the 199.6/160-mm paths, 1,520 binary writes, 18 support groups,
380 independent bank/patch alignment counts, zero-work limit, motion crossover,
strict-threshold neighbors, an independent motion-leg sum and budget recomposition.
The finite full-map deck sum is `16 move(5)+2 move(3)+2 move(4)`
(94 mm), plus 76 row-pitch moves at B=4; proof slots and retries remain charged. Source controls reproduce
E-083's H=21 B=16 **27.40965 s** and E-050's **29.34998/23.87740 s** references.
The new allocation does not silently overwrite them. This finite accounting has
no time-step or mesh convergence claim. No purchase, fabrication or deeper
contact solver is justified for the rejected coupling.
