Use: a physical Dungeons & Dragons battle map. Dense independently height-adjustable square/rectangular columns form terrain, walls, stairs, pits and platforms while supporting normal miniatures.

Objective: minimize realistic total physical cost while maximizing reliability, durability, manufacturability, update speed, compactness, repairability and requirement coverage. Count printed material, printing burden and assembly effort even though printed parts have no formal cost ceiling.

Existing designs are baselines, not commitments. Validate replacements before displacing demonstrated capability. Requirements live in stage 02.
