# Final engineering disposition

15 October 2026. **No complete architecture in this investigation satisfies the combined affordability, playability, manufacturing and update requirements.** The recommended build is the small manual positive-rack support-transfer article. The resident two-bank topology is a conditional research option, not an approved full-display construction.

## What changed the decision

DES-007's motor sharing leaves 512 costly selectors in its favorable eight-head comparison. Its constructed-strip four-head schedule is too slow. Independent alternatives then showed why simply moving fewer selectors around the field also fails: programming travel, repeated masks and support transfers consume the saved time. Crossed support combs disturb unselected pins by changing their bearing datum. Prewritten target memory does not remove the physical retirement operation.

Stationary resident state removes traveling-head transactions. The proposed captured C-guide gives a concrete route from shared column data to separate dog capture and ground-pawl release. Its ideal signed pulse sequence can meet the representative scene budget at assumed rates. But detailed integration introduces thousands of precision slides and joins, tall columns, heavier programming bars, unresolved bank-end connections and an actual admitted-play jam. A bounded longer-guide/shorter-tooth revision removes the sampled planar jam, but vertical play consumes the nominal unloading allowance. Reducing active data actuators to 128 has not established an affordable machine.

## Strongest objections and disposition

| Review finding | Action / remaining classification |
|---|---|
| Nominal strip isolation omits elasticity and variation | DES-007 preserved as untested evidence; removed from preferred full-machine path |
| Scanned writers and remaining-active masks were credited unrealistically fast | Independent schedules include indexing and physical exchange; low-writer-count embodiments fail the benchmark |
| A single inverse dog/pawl gate has no supported overlap | Separate A and B functions retained; the same fixed pawl carries every unchanged output |
| Free and sticky pins respond differently to reversal backlash | Rejected early compensation. Ideal common sequence restores ground first, then seats 0.7 mm and centres 0.3 mm after both signs |
| Small manual fixture had trapped assembly, disappearing guide lands, bolt interference, root rocking and flexible load paths | Rebuilt with continuous pin lands, lower guide, removable bolt/sleeve stack, preload relief, stiff floor/lid/seat and tool access; final nominal and assembly checks pass |
| Manual individual dog retirement differs from a global A frame | Explicit limit: the article proves only observed manual support transfer. It does not qualify simultaneous automatic release across manufacturing variation |
| Earlier memory tilt bound omitted independent flag rotation | Captive shoulders added; general tolerance guarantee withdrawn; final physical priority deferred because the surviving writer is different |
| Resident top-only writer allows opposite end states | Paired upper/lower capture pockets introduced; additional bars, lugs, links and forces counted. Powered synchronization remains unqualified |
| A resident dog jams within admitted rigid guide play | Original embodiment fails; the bounded guide revision removes sampled planar jams but has no guaranteed vertical unloading margin |
| Abutting bank links collide with terrain | Explicit complete-bank joining obligation; a local cell drawing is not a complete array assembly |
| Lighter historical columns understate force and cost | Recomputed actual 14.93-kg column mass, 8.25-kg stationary channel mass and corrected timing independently |
| Common-base corridors fail despite finer pitch | Retained the exact wall-clearance failure. No smaller base, wider grid or altered wall interface is silently accepted |

## What the recommended article can decide

The P1 article contains four active racks and four witnesses. Its final source is `0d75dbccb58f0134b698a23b19b4550c2cabab21a4e8aa852bce8a03ef6c3964`. It uses ordinary PLA and ten M3 screw/nut joints, with no motor or special sheet material. The supplied plate contains 46 parts. The comparison slice is **52.12 g / 9 h 20 min 17 s**, supports off and 3-mm brim, after splitting the STL into its 46 objects without changing their orientation.

Independent checks cover 240 operating poses, 648 mixed-drag/individual-retirement poses, 2,048 guide-play cases and 686 assembly-path poses. They establish only the stated rigid geometry. Printed fit, spring return, frame motion, positive downward contact, loaded seating, unchanged witness heights and wear remain **unresolved physically**. Record instrument resolution and every unsuccessful combination. The owner instructions deliberately distinguish observed support from inferred head travel.

The short-flag memory article retains verified fabrication files and 83 nominal checks, but is **DEFERRED**. Its success would not qualify the tall paired-register writer; its failure could be specific to its printed fixture. A second print is not recommended merely because its CAD exists.

## Boundary before a larger machine

The next useful dependencies are measured support transfer and guide drag, a toleranced and quoteable neighboring resident module, a demonstrated complete OLD-to-NEW row transaction under the actual programming load, and an accepted playable wall/base interface. At 64², the corrected sampled scene gates are approximately 107/106 ms per complete row for 40/50-mm travel. Dense and neutral-preparation cases still exceed 30 seconds. At 0.5 N mean drag per moving site the comparison drive fails its torque envelope; a hand-friendly isolated pin is insufficient evidence of aggregate load.

The conventional construction already allocates about $2,310–2,830 before custom resident mechanisms. Cheaper transmissions, fabricated selector electronics or molding may change that number, but have no qualified process or quote here. A materially higher budget, smaller field, slower updates or changed wall interface would be an explicit owner trade-off. None is assumed in the recommendation.

No new physical test, purchase, full-array fabrication or merge into `main` is claimed. This is a negative full-machine result with a concrete small experimental handoff and reproducible reasons, not digital product success.
