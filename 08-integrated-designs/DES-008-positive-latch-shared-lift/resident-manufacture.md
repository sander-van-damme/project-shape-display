# Resident mechanism: manufacture and assembly limits

Companion to [resident-integration.md](resident-integration.md); same frozen comparison geometry.

## Mass, stiffness, force and repeated hardware

`analysis/check_resident.py` computes the values from dimensions and exported meshes. The solid reference pin is about3.645 g PLA, **14.93 kg for4096**, before support hardware. This is intentionally not the earlier light hollow-column estimate. The channel has1.84 mm² steel section, about1.964 g before end bridges/tabs, **8.046 kg for the bare sections**. The actual joined channel mesh, including register feet and both programming lugs, is256.632 mm³,2.01456 g/site and **8.25164 kg total**; use that larger mass for selection-frame accounting. Its mass is in the laterally shifted selection frame, not the Z terrain lift, except transient row acquisition. Each addressed64-channel row already contains about126 g of channel metal.

With assumed steel E200 GPa, a134-mm end-supported channel gives0.178 mm/N ideal lateral compliance. At0.25 N this is0.0445 mm. The same section in PLA at3 GPa gives11.86 mm/N: an unsupported printed channel fails. End-register contact compliance, join flexibility and torsion are omitted and must increase the real bound. A203.2-mm steel rail with32 evenly loaded sites has roughly0.061 mm vertical deflection at1 N/site; concentrated and eccentric loads, end-joint flexibility and torsion are extra.

The reference40-mm steel return leaf gives0.051 N at0.85-mm release before friction. A45° sliding ramp with assumedμ0.2 requires roughly0.0765 N vertical input for that spring alone. All2048 selected sites therefore require approximately157 N on one B frame before guide friction and reserve. B is a powered load-bearing sequencer, not an instantaneous data operation. Common A must move roughly4 kg of channel steel per bank plus memory frames, writer hardware, friction and inertia.

For the full4096-site machine, count at least4096 pins,4096 channels,4096 A keys,4096 B keys,4096 grounded pawls,8192 key-guide interfaces,8192 positive end-register interfaces (16,384 pockets),8192 writer lugs,8192 codebar acquisition pockets,4096 local rigid pawl seats, and8192 channel end bridges/join operations. Some can be integrated into combs or plates, but those contacts and their tolerance requirements remain. Add128 data actuators,64 row cassettes,256 pocketed codebars and256 exterior paired links in the isolated-bank reference,128 A rails,128 B rails, two A subframes, two B ladders, two Z lift ladders and their external drives/guides/arrests. This is **not** a cheap architecture demonstrated by counting only128 selectors.

## Assembly and honest unresolved interfaces

The present connected channel solid includes end bridges. The A and B T-feet must be inserted from an open end **before** the second bridge is joined, or the channel must be manufactured with a removable/attachable face. The model does not qualify welding, brazing, crimping, an adhesive joint or a serviceable fastening process at this scale. The lower programming lug and bridge add another joining/feature obligation; pairing bars adds real moving mass, alignment and guides, not free synchronization. There are no concealed removable screws in the CAD. Thousands of unquoted precision joins are a serious manufacturing obstacle. The rear wall/lips cannot simply be omitted to ease assembly because they provide the positive capture.

**Bank nesting remains unresolved.** The reference CAD shows writer-pair links beyond BOTH ends of an isolated bank. Two contiguous32-row banks do not have a free exterior at their shared boundary. Independent exact-geometry review demonstrates14.688 mm³ intersection between the unmirrored writer end link and the next bank's pin: that directly abutting arrangement is FAILED, not merely an undocumented margin. Mirroring their channel sides away from that boundary, putting narrow independent ladder end beams in the3.95-mm tail gap and using writer links only at the outer display edges is a possible layout revision; it is not released geometry. Do not count the isolated-bank render as a completed contiguous4096-site frame.

During writing, the end pockets are disengaged. The upper/lower codebar pair must therefore keep the two endpoints aligned while channel inertia, sliding T-foot drag and friction act. The static end-supported channel calculation is not a writing stiffness qualification. A20-ms1.6-mm triangular move implies16 m/s², and a bare channel contributes about0.032 N inertia before key friction. The exported32-pocket paired writer reference is3430.506 mm³ steel,26.9295 g/pair or **3.447 kg for128 pairs**, including its two exterior link blocks. Moving each such pair1.6 mm in20 ms adds **0.431 N of inertial force per setter**, before the selected channel, sliding friction, return spring and impact. This is a substantial fraction of the catalog solenoid force anchor and is not qualified by a static force headline. Pair-link bending, guidance, two-lug phase error and settling within0.10-mm pocket clearance remain unqualified;80 ms is still a timing assumption.

Likewise the0.35-mm key/0.45-mm guide is a micro slide with0.10-mm walls, not a printable sturdy housing. Nominal neighbor clearance at far OFF is0.30 mm. Guide translation, yaw, frame bending and fabrication error consume this. The independent reviewer is checking exact rigid-body play; the nominal Boolean pass must not be promoted to a tolerance pass. A guide redesign or a coarser mechanism plane may be necessary, and would change pitch/stack/cost.

Broad upper bodies have two fixed guide planes at100/130 mm. At the highest cap, a0.20-mm total guide clearance can permit roughly0.95 mm relative approach of two neighbors before elastic deflection or yaw. A4.8-mm cap leaves1.55 mm nominal gap. Assumed0.25-N lateral loads on the56.2-mm overhang add about0.22 mm paired elastic deflection at E3 GPa. This is a bounded geometry screen, not a playability or print-yield qualification; guide plates are not released CAD. Broad bodies, cap gaps and upper guidance must be tested together.
