Numerical evidence

  targets in the ideal stopped-mask model; unchanged channels remain unchanged.
  Timing symmetry/zero/acceleration/rewind anchors and contact bounds checked.
- CAD: 1,540 structure pairs and 200 screw/pin positions (25 per output) with
  zero positive-volume intersection. Correct helical phase is essential:
  the deliberately wrong-phase witness intersects by 15.528512 mm^3.
- All 16 ideal taut-strip masks retain nominal 0.30 mm OFF clearance.
  This is not a flexible-strip dynamics simulation or measured tolerance.
- Offset-link/pressure-tip envelope checked at both selector endpoints and
  13 withdrawal heights through 12 mm, against adjacent rows/columns and both
  wheel tiers: zero intersection. A straight-stem negative control intersects
  by 7.002172 mm^3. The withdrawal check is sampled, not a continuous sweep proof.

  adhesive fillets, elastic deformation, roughness and printed variation are
  not solid-tolerance-stack qualifications. Collars/keeper deliberately have
  0.2 mm float; journals 0.1 mm radial and guides 0.15 mm side clearance.
- STL export removes degenerate facets, merges vertices and normalizes winding;
  no hole filling. All supplied part meshes watertight, positive volume.
  PLA plate 215 x 162.450001 x 50 mm, 76 disconnected objects,
  solid CAD volume 41405.824518 mm^3. TPU 180 x 3 x 0.2 mm, 108 mm^3.

Slicer evidence

  10h47m59s. Includes brim and support; not X1C elapsed time.
- TPU comparison: 58.92 mm filament, 0.14 cm^3, 0.17 g, 1m16s;
  one 0.2 mm layer. Heating, calibration/purge and actual handling additional.
- 819293 positive-XY PLA extrusion segments parsed. Support midpoints within
  the specified screw-thread cylinders: zero. This limited spatial test does
  not prove all support can be removed or exclude nozzle/scarring risks.
- Actual layer plots at Z0.2,22.1,24.05,30.05 inspected. Screw bases have brims;
  sacrificial posts brace collars below the final 20 mm threaded cantilevers.
  Cleanup must avoid thread, journal and thrust-seat surfaces. The threaded
  upright geometry remains a real physical print risk, not a qualified yield.
- Initial TPU profile failed validation at 100% nonrectilinear infill; corrected
