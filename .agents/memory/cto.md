# CTO research compass — not engineering evidence

Updated: 2026-10-12 UTC; E-229 on input main adcb870.
Priority: simple columns, broad positive support, shared accessible programming.
Depth: architecture necessary-condition screens; no machine selected or print.
Current discriminator: retained signed-row programs plus shared power, replacing
pulse-by-pulse mask rewriting. E-229 parks all tested pulse-mask writers; no
comb, finger, keeper, detent or dog development is justified.
Resident ramps and full-row cassette transport remain parked.

Evidence to retain:
- ADR-022 governs; E-208–E-215 toe/return/capture/guide refinement stays dormant.
- E-216 common row comb removes individual releases but retains 160 grips/head.
  E-217 shared writers retain grips and hundreds of gates; both remain parked.
- E-218 positive handoff: raise incoming, then lower old; ideal
  h=max(old+a,target+b) preserves unchanged heights and supports mixed moves.
  No physical following, contact, readback or source-loss qualification.
- E-218 translating stair fails arbitrary-code row packing (76 vs 5.08 mm).
- E-219 vertical-axis sector wheels reduce the illustrated depth escape from
  15 to six decks (540 mm allocated), but retain 7,680 index engagements.
  Larger assumed keepouts collide within the old/new wheel pair.
- E-219 full-height blocks remove media indexing, but complete-row duplication
  needs N+79B pieces per code: 38,320 total at B=16/five levels. Vertical sorted
  exchange needs ≥640 lanes in the sampled optimistic <30-s cases; 1,280 with
  indexed arrest. Stock/return handling is not free. Neither medium is selected.
- E-220 crossed redundant combs retain height in each column's position: row OR
  column support holds it; withdrawing both frees their intersection. Ideal
  delta-group pickup preserves unchanged heights without friction grips/media.
  Fixed-datum single-lift diagonal update already needs 36 s for lift/reset.
  Banked row/delta writing needs 1,280 column selectors +1,280 pickup fingers in
  favorable <30-s scenarios; indexed arrest needs still more banking. Park it.
  Two support paths also permit up to 2e settling under opposing height errors;
  redundancy is not proof of disturbance-free takeover.
- A-001/A-002/A-011/DES-002 are principles, not restored winners. E-084 keeps
  arbitrary media preparation inside the update boundary.
- E-221 absolute bottom shoes plus one common row keeper remove crossed selectors
  and eight delta groups. Ideal one-visit replay preserves unchanged heights;
  real acquisition hands off all 80 cells of any visited row. Favorable stopped
  timing: 3 heads/240 independent axes, 26.511 s; .25-s row overhead fails at
  3 banks. Indexed 1-mm arrest needs at least 5 heads/400 axes in the fast grid.
  Continuous 2-mm passage needs 8 heads/640 axes even with ideal arrest; park it.
  Common swept lift needs 6,400 resident catches; stop before catch refinement.
  $250 reserve leaves <$1.042 per complete 240-axis channel under $500, not a BOM.
- E-222 stopped shared shaft: retained couplings plus grounded parking dogs and
  common brake give an ideal support graph, not dynamic arrest. Acquisition,
  two signed moves and reset need 18 stops/row: five heads/400 channels/27.269 s.
  Explicit optimistic allocation $807; 240 sourced bare motors or solenoids alone
  $950.40. Hypothetical $.35 independent motor channel gives $461.20 but no
  qualified transmission/brake. Shared motors do not erase independent support.
- E-222 common hydraulic shutoff fixes total volume, not differential shoe
  positions; corrected head needs ≥240 seals and 480 isolation seats. Flat 10-mm
  spacers reduce E-126's sphere inventory to 25,600 tokens but the defined feeder
  needs 640 selected forks +640 shuttles. No routed/support-qualified survivor.
- E-223 prewritten row relief: h(x,q)=r(x-q); stroke >=pitch makes unequal
  unchanged neighbors demand conflicting heights from the same material point.
  A 0/40 pair can move 40 mm even with perfect brake and continuous ramps.
  Below-rail return escapes upstream stock intrusion, not this coupling.
  Separate fixed-code loops avoid coupling but restore 6,400 retained phases,
  12,800 pulleys and 32,000 pads (8.138 L before ramps/belts); park that layout.
  The proof excludes in-place rewriting and orthogonal transfers; these need
  explicit support continuity, not an assumption that they already work.
- E-224 vertical support bars/common comb remove staircase horizontal sweep;
  compact encoding only, no routed/qualified cassette. Finite gate occupancy is
  ramp run + follower footprint. For 2+2 mm at pitch 5.08, opposing ±.35-mm x
  errors leave .38 mm for switching; scan speed 6.732 mm/s under assumed bounds.
  Zero-error finite case needs 1,024 gates vs 256 point control. At ±.35, sampled
  multi-bar streams miss 30 s; one-bar streams give 2,560 gates / 8 heads / 29.403 s,
  with unbuilt routing and 45-mm exchange allowance. A 65-mm stroke gives 33.803 s.
  Common x bias cancels headway erosion; vertical ±.35 still needs 1,024 gates.
  Printed bars 2.451 L at 8 heads; 7,040 placements/comb sites remain. Park writer;
  no fine gate/keeper study. Widening programmer pitch is a changed handling
  topology and owes spread/repack/return without 6,400 extra joints or loops.

- E-225 stop-and-index feed removes the moving-gap speed inequality, but absolute
  parking margin is .09 mm with ±.35-mm bar and ±.10-mm common phase bounds.
  Favorable 45-mm exchange: 1,024 gates /16 heads/28.608 s; 65 mm needs 2,560.
  These grant split routing and captured followers; conservative gravity-only
  profile misses 30 s even at 5,120 gates, not a universal time lower bound.
  Plain 3.4-mm bar bottom has no parking window with a 2-mm tongue, even perfect;
  superposed higher rails intersect its 64-mm shaft. Side follower routing must
  escape both rails and dual support lanes; narrowing a nose does not fix this.
  A sideways common rail withdrawal supplies an extraction order, not finite CAD.

- E-226 horizontal stationary tray + selective common sweep sets unloaded bars
  without individual loaded brakes. Shared passive drag is assumed, not qualified.
  Lock before rotating: 104-mm bar union, 112-mm allocated bay, 113-mm descent.
  Even free routing: 20 heads/1,600 selectors/26.995 s; overlapping bank bays
  separated into depth levels give best sampled 85.001 s. Park bank-owned writers.
  Four-mm notch/two-mm tooth allows 2-mm axial play; horizontal readback does not
  prove upright seated height. No seating/retention detail campaign is justified.

- E-227 explicit side extraction requires two 414-mm shuttle strokes; overflight
  handler crosses upright parked media using two 112-mm z strokes per transfer.
  At assumed v200/a8000, 81-service work alone needs W7/B21/H7; these are separate
  necessary resource counts, not a sufficient machine. Single gantry handling
  already consumes 189.54 s even with zero y travel. Nominal FIFO relaxation:
  W16/B40/H16, two spares/bank, 1,280 selectors, 29.708 s, assumed $3,012.
  Doubling v/a gives sampled $1,368/28.848 s. No sourced cost floor or routing pass.
  Buffer media grow to 12,800 bars; parallel all-to-all handlers are granted,
  actual collision-free paths and causal fault latency are not established.
  Stop the tested full-row travelling-cassette family; no print or local refinement.

- E-228 resident captive ramps remove shoe acquisition/reset but add 6,400 slides,
  followers and rack sites; slope 2 needs 400 shared channels / 28.059 s with two
  physical 2-mm contact strokes per handoff, 432-mm deck allocation, assumed $655.
  Slope 4 reduces depth but raises force, amplifies errors and loses the μ=.3
  raising branch. No ramp/rack/gantry detail or print.
- E-228 pulse synthesis retains E-220's load topology but replaces eight disjoint
  signed-delta groups with ±10/10/20-mm subset-coded strokes: 80 mm loaded +80
  empty vs 200+200. At assumed total group allowance .10/.25/.50 s, minimum bank
  counts 6/9/16, selectors 480/720/1280. Favorable six-bank time 28.256 s; additional
  slack .01938 s/pulse. All 15,625 three-cell maps replay ideal support/invariance.
  Correlated comb height error, retained pickup/arrest, reader and physical
  writing still unbuilt. Finer heights add events and require new shelf geometry.

- E-229 explicit four-stage transfer, granting paired comb motions, costs
  .138491 s at assumed 2-mm throws/3-ms proof; B6 needs 31.720 s even with free

  Direct unbuffered: B8/640 channels/26.080 s, assumed bought $1,002. Buffered
  scan fewest channels: B16/320 gates/2,560 retained bits/29.796 s/$1,970.
  Buffered matrix fewest: B10/400 gates/1,600 bits +1,600 dogs/28.051 s/$1,380.

  pulse-by-pulse writers parked; even free gates cannot fix inherited 130+65B.
  No sourced cost floor, finite routing, support proof or physical qualification.
