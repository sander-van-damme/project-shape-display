## Retain older principles without reviving failed embodiments

A-001/A-002/A-011/DES-002 are starting principles, not restored winners. E-084
keeps media writing inside update time. Jacquard/shared physical programming still
has strategic value; selection alone does not supply load, height, readback or
recovery. A materially changed support/topology/process can reopen a principle.

E-216–E-242 cover row grips, swapped programs, stops, ramps, pulse synthesis and
physical writers: repeated load transfer, real program rewrite, capture/retention,

column tilts/turns, staggered carriers and continuous crank encodings. Regional
