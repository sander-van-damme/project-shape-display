# Selectively coupled multistable scissor stacks

**Bounded; deep planar scissor embodiment deprioritized.** Descriptors: local
bistable coupling flags on shared lateral rails; geometry/over-centre stops as
memory; common lateral motor power; locked links/guide/frame load path; serial
rotational stages per pin. Kirigami, origami and buckled structures motivate
topology search, but naming them does not establish 40-mm travel.

Each guided top sits on a serial stack of crossed links. Shared lateral combs
change stack width only when a locally addressed magnetic dog engages its
slider. Positive catches at five stack extensions retain the chosen height;
over-centre springs assist capture but do not alone certify load support.
Engage the slider, unload/open its catch, move width inward/outward to raise/
lower, re-engage catch, proof support and disengage the dog. Unchanged stacks
remain caught and decoupled from the comb. The comb must return without dragging
uncoupled sliders. Row/column flag pulses and shield/threshold gates are as in
A-016; all stack sliders must be kinematically synchronized, not independently
assumed to follow a common rail.

E-063 generates 1,944 planar geometries over bar length, angles, stage count
and clearance envelopes. Only 163 pass pitch/travel; minimum 13 stages uses
26 bars per site, about 166,400 bars/board, before pivots and catches. The
13-stage example needs 73.7 N lateral force per 1 N vertical load near its
low-angle state; 6,400 simultaneous 1-N loads imply approximately 472 kN
lateral reaction in that ideal synchronized geometry. High-angle mechanical
advantage does not remove the starting singularity. Distributed actuators or
pressure along stages change the embodiment and accounting.

The sweep checks straight-line link width/height through monotone angle and
finite clearance budget, not 3D pivots, contacts or actual bistability. Minimum
13 stages applies to this grid, not all compliant geometries. Printed hinges
would repeat large strain and orientation-dependent fatigue; purchased pivots
would need <$0.00150 each for 26/site at $250 reserve before any other purchase.
Assembly and inaccessible link repair are worse than simple prismatic tails.

Read top/tail position and latch flag, then proof unload the comb. Failed
snap/capture retains comb support and stops the module; a removable pin-stack
cartridge isolates repair. Local buckling can invade neighbor clearance;
common comb deflection can half-release multiple stacks. No device-scale
throughput is established: five width dwells can fit the shared-drive time
allocation only if force and capture are resolved, which this embodiment fails
to demonstrate. Stop further parameter refinement of planar 13+ stage stacks.
Reopen with a different deployed-volume topology, wider staggered base, tension
member, or materially lower reaction path, not a finer angle grid.

[Yang, Dias and Holmes](https://arxiv.org/abs/1807.06498) demonstrate locally
reversible multistable kirigami; this is a topology lead, not evidence that
small printed stacks bear tabletop loads. Accessed 2026-10-08.
