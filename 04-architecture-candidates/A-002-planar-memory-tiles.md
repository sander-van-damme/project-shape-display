Removable perforated stop layers, reusable aperture sheets or profile strips encode heights outside columns. A shared lift reads passive stops; independently serviced tiles support regional changes. Four aperture planes plus bottom stop can encode five heights.

Risks: multilayer registration, follower catching, exchange disturbance, writer throughput and surprise-map latency. Slow prepared media is a separate product mode; double buffering does not establish sustained <30 s arbitrary-map updates.


Research disposition: comparison reference under ADR-009. Use the operating principle as a search seed; further work requires a distinct mechanism or decision-changing computational result. No automatic coupon handoff.
